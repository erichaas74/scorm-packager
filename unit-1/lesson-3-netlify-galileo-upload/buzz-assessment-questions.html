Buzz Assessment Questions: Galileo Acceleration Lab

Assessment Setup

Assessment title Galileo Acceleration Lab Analysis
Total points 10 points plus screenshot upload evidence, or 11 points if you want the upload worth 1 point.
Question type Use Essay for Questions 1-6 so BusyBee can grade the written responses. Add one final upload question for the completed chart screenshot.
Template file Upload buzz-assessment-template-for-buzz.html after creating the questions in this exact order.

Question 1: Interval Distance Trend

Type: Essay

Points: 2

Prompt: Describe the trend in the interval distance column. What does that trend tell you about the object's speed?

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit: explains that interval distances increase over equal time intervals and connects that increase to the object moving faster as it falls.

Question 2: Evidence of Acceleration

Type: Essay

Points: 2

Prompt: Explain how you can tell from the data that the object was accelerating during the fall.

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit: states that acceleration means velocity changes, then uses increasing interval distances or increasing speed over equal time intervals as evidence.

Question 3: Interval Ratio Pattern

Type: Essay

Points: 1.5

Prompt: Compare your interval ratio column to Galileo's odd-number pattern: 1, 3, 5, 7, and so on.

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit: recognizes that interval ratios should be close to odd numbers and explains that this is evidence of constant acceleration.

Question 4: Total Distance Ratio Pattern

Type: Essay

Points: 1.5

Prompt: Compare your total distance ratio column to the square-number pattern: 1, 4, 9, 16, and so on.

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit: recognizes that total distance ratios should be close to square numbers and connects this to distance growing with time squared.

Question 5: Sources of Error

Type: Essay

Points: 1.5

Prompt: Identify at least one source of error in the digital experiment and explain how it could affect the data.

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit: names a plausible error source and explains its effect on measurement or interpretation.

Question 6: Galileo's Tools

Type: Essay

Points: 1.5

Prompt: Explain why Galileo's water clocks, bells, or inclined planes could introduce more uncertainty than a modern digital simulation or video measurement.

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit: explains that Galileo's tools had limited precision and human timing or measurement uncertainty, then compares that to the more precise timing and repeatability of digital tools.

Question 7: Completed Chart Screenshot

Type: File Upload if available, or Essay/Custom question with attachment upload enabled.

Points: 0 for evidence only, or 1 if you want to grade completion.

Prompt: Upload a screenshot of the completed Galileo data chart from the lab website.

Grading guide

Full credit: screenshot clearly shows the completed chart with all time rows and all four calculated columns filled in.
