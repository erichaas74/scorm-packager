Lab Format Suggestions: Unit 1 Lesson 1 - Motion Graphs Lab

Current fit for the new Buzz format
- This lesson should stay as a SCORM activity, not a native Buzz-question assessment.
- The built-in 15-question assessment remains inside the lab.
- The lab now reports completion and score to Buzz through SCORM.
- All assessment questions are multiple choice inside the lab.
- The assessment section no longer reveals correct answers after wrong attempts.
- The question dropdown marks unanswered questions, completed questions, and questions that need retry.

Current SCORM behavior
- Score reports as points earned out of 15.
- Completion reports only after all 15 questions have been attempted.
- Attempt history is saved in SCORM suspend_data.
- Students can retry questions; the best score per question counts.
- Students can click "Submit / Resubmit to Buzz" after completion, and the score also syncs after each answer.

Remaining work before publishing
1. Upload the packaged lesson-1.zip to Buzz as a SCORM activity.
2. Set the Buzz gradebook score to 15 points.
3. Enable relaunch/resubmission if students should be allowed to improve their score.
4. Preview as a student and confirm Buzz receives completion after all 15 questions are attempted.
5. Confirm Buzz displays the raw score on a 0-15 scale.
6. Keep native Buzz questions turned off for this lab.
7. Confirm the dropdown status markers are visible in the target browser/Buzz frame.
