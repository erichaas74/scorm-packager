Buzz Setup Notes: Unit 1 Lesson 1 - Motion Graphs Lab

Important setup decision
- Do not recreate the 15 built-in questions as native Buzz questions.
- The questions are generated and graded inside the activity.
- Upload this lesson as a SCORM activity in Buzz.
- Buzz should receive the completed/incomplete status and the student's score as points earned out of 15.

SCORM reporting behavior
- Before all 15 questions are attempted: lesson_status = incomplete.
- After all 15 questions are attempted: lesson_status = completed.
- Score is reported as cmi.core.score.raw out of cmi.core.score.max = 15.
- Students can retry questions in the activity.
- The best score for each question counts.
- After retries, the score is synced again automatically and students can also click "Submit / Resubmit to Buzz" on the completion card.
- All assessment questions are presented as multiple choice inside the activity.
- Wrong assessment attempts tell students to retry but do not reveal the correct answer.
- The question dropdown marks each question as [TODO], [OK], or [RETRY].

Buzz assignment setup
- Activity type: SCORM package.
- Upload file: lesson-1.zip after packaging this folder.
- Grade source: SCORM score.
- Suggested grade scale: 15 points.
- Allow students to relaunch/resubmit if you want them to improve their score.

Student-facing direction
Open the Motion Graphs Lab, switch to Assessment mode, answer all 15 built-in questions, and submit/resubmit your score to Buzz from the completion card.
