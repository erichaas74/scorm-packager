Buzz Setup Notes: Unit 1 Lesson 1 - Motion Graphs Lab

Important setup decision
- Do not recreate the lab questions as native Buzz questions.
- The randomized questions, graph choices, retries, and grading run inside the application.
- Upload lesson-1.zip to Buzz as a SCORM 1.2 activity.
- Buzz receives a completed/incomplete status and a raw score from 0 to 15 points.

Assessment behavior
- The application contains 15 required core questions and 5 optional bonus questions.
- Before all 15 core questions are attempted: lesson_status = incomplete.
- After all 15 core questions are attempted: lesson_status = completed.
- Each correct answer earns one point. Bonus points can replace missed core points, but the reported score is capped at 15.
- Students can retry questions; the best score per question counts.
- Attempted/correct state is saved in cmi.suspend_data and restored when the Buzz activity is relaunched.
- The score syncs after every answer and when the student selects Submit / Resubmit to Buzz.
- Wrong attempts tell students to retry without revealing the answer.

Buzz assignment setup
- Activity type: SCORM package.
- SCORM version: 1.2.
- Upload file: ../lesson-1.zip.
- Grade source: SCORM score.
- Gradebook points possible: 15.
- Allow relaunch/resubmission if students may improve their score.

Student-facing direction
Open the Motion Graphs Lab, choose Assessment, answer all 15 core questions, and use the 5 optional bonus questions to recover missed points. After completing the core questions, select Submit / Resubmit to Buzz.
