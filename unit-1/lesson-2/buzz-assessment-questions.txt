Buzz Assessment Setup: Unit 1 Lesson 2 - City Blocks Challenge

This is a SCORM completion activity. Passing a level = building a route that
matches both of that level's targets. There are no built-in quiz questions.

What the activity contains
- 8 route-building levels on the city grid. Every move takes 1 minute, and a
  WAIT button lets a minute pass at a stoplight without moving.
- Each level has exactly two targets:
  Level 1: distance 6, displacement 2.
  Level 2: distance 8, displacement 4.
  Level 3: distance 8, displacement 0.
  Level 4: distance 9, displacement 5.
  Level 5: distance 11, displacement 5.
  Level 6: distance 6, average speed 0.75 blocks/min (forces 2 waits).
  Level 7: distance 8, average speed 0.8 blocks/min (forces 2 waits).
  Level 8: displacement 5, average velocity 0.5 blocks/min (10-minute trip).
- The HUD dashboard shows the two targets next to the student's current
  values; live average speed/velocity readouts appear only on levels 6-8.
- A level dropdown lets students play levels in any order and marks each
  level ○ (not passed) or ✓ (passed).

Scoring and completion (SCORM 1.2)
- Score scale: 0-8 points, 1 per level passed.
- cmi.core.lesson_status is only ever "incomplete" or "completed".
  It becomes "completed" when all 8 levels are passed. The activity never
  reports passed/failed, so it works as a completion activity in Buzz.
- Progress resumes across sessions via cmi.suspend_data.

Buzz setup steps
1. Upload unit-1/lesson-2.zip as a SCORM package.
2. Set the gradebook score to 8 points (or grade on completion only).
3. Allow relaunch so students can resume; suspend data restores progress.
4. Preview and confirm Buzz shows "completed" only after all 8 levels.

Optional evidence question (only if you want redundancy in Buzz)
Type: File upload
Prompt: Upload a screenshot of the City Blocks Challenge showing the
"All Levels Complete!" banner and the Levels 8 / 8 readout.
Grading guide: Full credit if the banner and readout are visible.

Optional concept questions for Buzz (native, if you want written assessment)
1. Essay: Explain how a trip can have a distance of 8 blocks but a
   displacement of 0 blocks. (Level 3 or 7 route.)
2. Essay: On level 6 you had to wait at stoplights. Explain why waiting
   lowers your average speed even though your route did not change.
3. Short answer: Your level 8 trip had displacement 5 blocks and took 10
   minutes. Show the calculation for your average velocity.
4. Essay: When are average speed and average velocity magnitude equal?
   When are they different? Use one of your routes as an example.
