Unit 1 Lesson 2: City Blocks Challenge

Use the self-contained Buzz template for the embedded challenge and native assessment questions.

Buzz setup

  1. Upload u1l2-city-blocks-buzz-assessment-template.html as the assessment template.
  2. Paste buzz-assessment-questions.txt into Buzz's Text editor or Edit all questions screen.
  3. Keep question order locked. Turn off randomization and one-question-per-page.
  4. Set the assessment total to 11 points. Allow retakes if students may improve their written responses.
  5. Ask students to complete the levels, download the evidence image, and upload it to Question 6.
  6. Use the BusyBee metadata in busybee-rubric-metadata.json for the essay and evidence review.

Preview checks

  • Confirm the square city canvas and HUD fit without overlap.
  • Confirm completed levels advance automatically and the dropdown marks passed levels green.
  • Confirm the evidence report updates when routes and levels change.
  • Confirm both evidence buttons work inside the Buzz preview.
