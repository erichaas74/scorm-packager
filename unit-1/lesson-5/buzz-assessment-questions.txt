Buzz Assessment Questions: Unit 1 Lesson 5 - Skydiving Motion Graphs

Use these as native Buzz questions after students run sky-dive-lab.html.

1. Type: Fill in the Blank
   Points: 1
   Prompt: In Trial A, what was the estimated terminal velocity during spread fall? Enter your answer in m/s.
   Answer: Accept the value recorded from the lab data table, with a tolerance of +/- 3 m/s.

2. Type: Fill in the Blank
   Points: 1
   Prompt: In Trial B, what was the estimated terminal velocity during the dive pose? Enter your answer in m/s.
   Answer: Accept the value recorded from the lab data table, with a tolerance of +/- 3 m/s.

3. Type: Multiple Choice
   Points: 1
   Prompt: Which body position produced the greater terminal velocity?
   Options:
   A. Trial A: spread fall
   B. Trial B: dive pose
   C. They were the same
   Answer: B

4. Type: Multiple Choice
   Points: 1
   Prompt: Why does the dive pose have a greater terminal velocity than the spread fall?
   Options:
   A. The diver has less air resistance, so a greater speed is needed before drag balances weight.
   B. The diver has more mass during the dive.
   C. Gravity becomes stronger during the dive.
   D. The parachute opens sooner during the dive.
   Answer: A

5. Type: Fill in the Blank
   Points: 1
   Prompt: In Trial C, at what time did the parachute deploy?
   Answer: 10 s

6. Type: Multiple Choice
   Points: 1
   Prompt: What happens to the acceleration just after the parachute opens?
   Options:
   A. It becomes upward/negative because drag is greater than weight for a short time.
   B. It stays at +9.8 m/s^2.
   C. It becomes zero instantly and stays exactly zero.
   D. It becomes greater downward because the parachute adds weight.
   Answer: A

7. Type: Multiple Choice
   Points: 1
   Prompt: On a velocity-time graph, terminal velocity is shown by which feature?
   Options:
   A. A horizontal section where velocity is nearly constant
   B. A vertical line
   C. A line that curves upward faster and faster forever
   D. A point where time goes backward
   Answer: A

8. Type: Fill in the Blank
   Points: 1
   Prompt: In Trial C, what was the landing speed after the parachute opened? Enter your answer in m/s.
   Answer: Accept the value recorded from the lab data table, with a tolerance of +/- 2 m/s.

9. Type: Essay
   Points: 3
   Prompt: Compare the velocity-time graphs for Trial A, Trial B, and Trial C. Explain how air resistance and the parachute changed the slope and final speed.
   Grading guide: Full credit should mention that slope represents acceleration, velocity levels off near terminal velocity, the dive has a greater terminal velocity because drag is lower, and the parachute causes a sharp decrease in speed before a slower final fall.

10. Type: File Upload
    Points: 2
    Prompt: Upload your evidence image from the Skydiving Motion Graphs lab.
    Grading guide: Evidence should show the lab graph and recorded data table for the completed trials.
