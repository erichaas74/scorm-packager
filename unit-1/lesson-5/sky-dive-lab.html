Unit 1 Lesson 5: Skydiving Motion Graphs

Run fixed skydiving trials, compare the velocity-time and acceleration-time graphs, and use the recorded values to answer the Buzz assessment questions.

Student Directions

  1. Run Trial A, Trial B, and Trial C. Let each trial finish or click Record Current Trial after the graph levels out.
  2. Use Velocity Graph and Acceleration Graph to compare how the shape changes during free fall, dive position, and parachute deployment.
  3. Copy the Buzz Data Summary into your notes, then answer the Buzz questions.
  4. Download the evidence image if your teacher asks for a screenshot upload.
