Buzz Setup: Unit 1 Lesson 4 Reaction Time Kinematics Lab

What this activity is

The assessment template shows the lab's three levels one after another down the page, and each level embeds its scenario with the original lab graphics and audio: the SVG hand and falling ruler with its full cm scale (Level 1), the perspective drag strip with the detailed dragsters, light tree, "Drivers, start your engines" voice cue, engine rev, and light beeps (Level 2), and the scrolling sprint track with articulated runners, the starter official, and the "On your marks… Set…" voice cues plus the starter-gun shot (Level 3). Each scenario starts from an on-scene Start overlay that tells the student to click it to begin and names that level's reaction signal (ruler drops / bottom light turns green / starter gun fires); the overlay hides during the trial so it never blocks the reaction click, then returns for the next trial. Below each scene are a Reset button, live readouts, a trial list, and a Copy trial data button, all driven by one shared script at the bottom of the template. Buzz cannot open linked/hosted files from a template, so everything lives inside the template itself. That level's Buzz questions render directly beneath its scenario. Per the conversion guide, only directions, redundant titles, and lab chrome (scoring, SCORM wiring, walkthrough, notebook validation) were trimmed from the original lab — the graphics were kept as built.

Measured reaction times are student-dependent, so they are never auto-graded. The auto-graded questions use fixed given values stated in each prompt (a 19.6 cm ruler drop; 0.400 s vs 0.250 s dragster reaction times on the 300 m strip; 0.150 s vs 0.300 s sprinter reaction times at 10 m/s), so every student grades against the same answer key. The four written-answer questions ask students to cite their own trials as evidence, and BusyBee grades them using busybee-rubric-metadata.json.

The scenarios never display the values students must calculate — same as the full lab's validation-gate design. Level 1 shows only the ruler drop (students calculate their reaction time from it); Level 2 shows both reaction times but not the finish gap (students calculate the gap); Level 3 shows the opponent's reaction time and the finish lag but not the student's own reaction time (students calculate it). Those readout chips permanently say "you calculate."

The template contains no formulas or solving help — operating directions only. The full lab (index.html, the SCORM version with the validation gate) keeps the Guide and Hint overlays for instruction before the assessment.

Step 1: Nothing to host

The template is fully self-contained — all three scenarios are embedded in it with their original graphics, so there is no separate simulator upload and no link to replace.

If Buzz strips the template's script (the scenes do not draw in preview) or rejects the template for size: do not cut graphics. Host the full lab (index.html) outside Buzz and switch the template to a hosted link or iframe, keeping the ten questions unchanged.

Step 2: Create the ten Buzz questions

  1. Create a Buzz Assessment titled Unit 1 Lesson 4: Reaction Time Kinematics Lab Analysis.
  2. Open buzz-assessment-questions.txt and paste the full contents into the Buzz Assessment text editor / Edit all questions screen. The file is pure Buzz text-editor syntax with no header notes, so it can be pasted whole.
  3. It creates ten questions worth 14 points in slot order: Level 1 gets Q1 (MC equation), Q2 (numeric), Q3 (essay); Level 2 gets Q4–Q5 (numeric), Q6 (essay); Level 3 gets Q7 (MC equation), Q8 (numeric), Q9–Q10 (essays). The essays' a. lines are BusyBee rubric summaries, not auto-graded keys.

Step 3: Upload the template

  1. Upload u1l4-reaction-time-buzz-assessment-template.html as the assessment template.
  2. The template has ten <a:question></a:question> slots; Buzz fills them with your ten questions in order, so the question order must match the slot order exactly.
  3. Open u1l4-reaction-time-buzz-assessment-template-preview.html locally first to see where each question lands.

CRITICAL: Buzz assessments default to RANDOMIZED question order, and the template fills its placeholders in display order — randomization puts questions under the wrong level's tester. In the assessment settings, turn OFF randomize/shuffle question order. Also do NOT set one question per page: each level's questions must render together beneath that level's tester.

Step 4: Preview as a student

  1. Confirm the page title reads Reaction Time Kinematics Lab Assessment. If it shows a different lab, the wrong template was uploaded.
  2. Confirm all three scenes draw with the original lab graphics (this verifies Buzz did not strip the template script): the hand and ruler with its cm scale, the drag strip with light tree and dragsters, and the sprint track with runners and the starter official.
  3. Confirm each scene shows its pulsing Start overlay naming that level's signal, that the overlay disappears once the trial starts, and that it returns after the trial ends. Run one trial in each: catch the ruler, launch on green, react to the gun.
  4. Confirm the solve targets stay hidden after each trial: Level 1 shows the drop but no reaction time, Level 2 shows both reaction times but no gap, and Level 3 shows the opponent RT and finish lag but not your reaction time. Those chips must read "you calculate."
  5. Confirm the audio cues play: "Drivers, start your engines" and the light beeps in Level 2, and "On your marks… Set…" plus the gunshot in Level 3 (the starter's muzzle flash is the visual fallback if audio is blocked).
  6. Confirm the questions land under the right levels: Q1–3 under Falling Ruler, Q4–6 under Drag Racing, Q7–10 under Elite Sprint. If they land in the wrong sections, question randomization is still on.
  7. Enter the answer-key values above and confirm the four numeric questions grade correct.
  8. Try a deliberate early click in each tester and confirm the false start / jump start / disqualified messages appear, and that reacting under 0.100 s in Level 3 is rejected.
  9. Confirm the Copy trial data buttons copy the recorded trial text.
