3 Discussion & Reflection

Galileo lacked modern stopwatches. He used water clocks and inclined planes to "dilute" gravity. Even with your smartphone, sources of error exist.

1. What sources of error were present in this digital experiment (camera angle, releasing the ball, etc.)?

2. How might a water clock or bells introduce more error than a 60fps video camera?
