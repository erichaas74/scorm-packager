How to use

  1. Open a tool, fill in your question, and upload images for each item.
  2. Click Download Full Package to get a zip with index.html and an assets folder.
  3. Extract the zip on your computer.
  4. Re-zip the contents (the index.html + the assets folder), then upload that new zip into a Rise custom block.
Runs entirely in your browser. No data is uploaded anywhere.
