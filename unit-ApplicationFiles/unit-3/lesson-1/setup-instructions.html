Buzz Setup: Forces and Motion Lab

Step 1: Confirm the PhET link

  1. Use the direct PhET simulation link: https://phet.colorado.edu/sims/html/forces-and-motion-basics/latest/forces-and-motion-basics_all.html.
  2. The Buzz template embeds the simulation and also includes an external launch button.
  3. Preview in Buzz. If the iframe does not load, students can still use the launch button.

Step 2: Create the Buzz assessment questions

  1. Create a Buzz Assessment titled Forces and Motion Lab.
  2. Create the nine questions listed in buzz-assessment-questions.txt.
  3. Keep the questions in the exact order shown. Each <a:question></a:question> slot pulls in the next Buzz question.

Step 3: Upload the Buzz template

  1. Upload buzz-assessment-template-for-buzz.html as the assessment template in Buzz.
  2. Preview as a student and confirm the PhET launch area, directions, and nine native Buzz questions appear.
