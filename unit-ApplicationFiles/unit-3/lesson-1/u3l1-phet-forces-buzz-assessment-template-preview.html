Student Directions

Objective: Use PhET Forces and Motion: Basics to investigate Newton's First and Second Laws.

Part 1: Net Force

  1. Select the Net Force screen.
  2. Turn on Sum of Forces, Values, and Speed.
  3. Place two small blue people on the left side of the cart and one big person on the right side of the cart.
  4. Press GO and observe the sum of forces, direction of the arrow, and velocity of the cart.
  5. Try balanced and unbalanced force combinations and compare how velocity changes.

Part 2: Motion

  1. Select the Motion screen.
  2. Turn on all display boxes in the upper right corner.
  3. Select the 200 kg refrigerator.
  4. Use the slider to apply force for a few seconds and observe the speed.
  5. Release the slider so the applied force returns to zero and observe the speed again.

A
