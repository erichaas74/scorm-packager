Student Directions

Objective: Use PhET Forces and Motion: Basics to investigate Newton's First and Second Laws.

Part 1: Net Force

  1. Select the Net Force screen.
  2. Turn on Sum of Forces, Values, and Speed.
  3. Place both 50 N blue pullers on the left side of the rope and the 150 N red puller on the right side.
  4. Press GO and observe the force totals, net-force arrow, and speed of the cart.
  5. Before taking your screenshot, make sure the setup still shows 100 N pulling left and 150 N pulling right.
  6. Then try at least one balanced setup and one other unbalanced setup. Compare how the cart's speed changes.

Part 2: Motion

  1. Select the Motion screen.
  2. Turn on all five display options: Force, Values, Masses, Speed, and Stopwatch.
  3. Remove the starting crate and place the Fridge on the skateboard. Confirm that its displayed mass is 200 kg.
  4. Use the applied-force control to apply 100 N to the right for a few seconds. Observe the speed while the force remains at 100 N.
  5. Return the applied force to 0 N and observe the speed again.
