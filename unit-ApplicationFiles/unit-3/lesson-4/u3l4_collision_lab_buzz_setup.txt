COLLISION LAB: MOMENTUM CONSERVATION
UNIT 3 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Run a five-crash momentum investigation! You will track objects that bounce, slow, or stick together and use completed before-and-after charts to uncover the quantity every collision protects.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Calculate signed momentum for objects moving left and right.
- Verify system momentum conservation across elastic and inelastic collisions.
- Explain zero total momentum, nonconserved velocity sums, and the effect of outside forces.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Run each of the five fixed collision trials.
2. Complete the two total-momentum entries in every provided trial chart.
3. Check signs carefully: right is positive and left is negative.
4. Use at least two completed trials as evidence in Question 11.
5. Use Trial 2 and outside-force reasoning in Question 12.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 12 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- This lesson uses 10 automatic points because students complete a provided data or measurement chart.
- No file upload earns or replaces points.
