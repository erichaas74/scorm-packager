📋 Trial Guide

  1. Check the trial's fixed starting conditions — masses show on the balls, initial velocities in the readouts, and the elasticity at the bottom of the sim. Everything is set for you.
  2. Press Run Collision and watch the labeled velocity arrows attached to each ball. After the collision the arrows keep showing the final velocities, even after a ball stops at a wall.
  3. Read the final velocities from the readouts — each shows a speed and a direction arrow.
  4. Record the values in your Buzz data chart, adding the signs yourself (see the Hint). Use your before/after totals to prove momentum is conserved and to see when kinetic energy is conserved.

💡 Hint: Direction Matters!

Every speed and momentum in this lab is shown as a magnitude with a direction arrow — the lab never shows + or − signs. You supply the sign yourself whenever you record or calculate.

  • Each trial's starting directions are fixed — read them from the arrows on the balls and in the readouts.
  • Momentum keeps the sign: p = mv.
  • Kinetic energy is always positive: KE = ½mv² (squaring removes the sign).
The sign rule: right (→) = positive (+), left (←) = negative (−).
