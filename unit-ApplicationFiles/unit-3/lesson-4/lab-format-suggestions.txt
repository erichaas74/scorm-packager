Unit 3 Lesson 4 - Collision Lab: Elastic and Inelastic Collisions
Remaining conversion and publishing work

Status: Buzz-native assessment, fixed numeric ready.
- The Buzz template is HTML/CSS only (no JavaScript) and is styled like a lab
  data chart. Questions 1-4 are separate numeric-input Buzz questions living
  inside the four chart cells; Question 5 is the analysis question.
- Recorded/set chart values (masses, initial velocities, elasticity) are
  filled in by the template as a static setup table; final velocities are
  read from the simulator readouts; the four calculated totals are the
  graded numeric questions.
- Numeric grading is possible because Trial 1 has a required fixed setup
  (m1=1.0 kg, v1=+3.0 m/s, m2=2.0 kg, v2=0, e=100% -> v1'=-1.00, v2'=+2.00,
  p=+3.00 kg*m/s and KE=4.50 J before and after).
- The simulator is now a pure sim: the learning-goals card, the fill-in data
  chart card, and the lab-evidence card were removed from collison-lab.html
  and exist only in the Buzz template.
