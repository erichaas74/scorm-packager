Unit 3 Lesson 4 - Collision Lab: Elastic and Inelastic Collisions
Remaining conversion and publishing work

Status: Buzz-native assessment, fixed numeric ready.
- The Buzz template is fully self-contained: each of the five trial sections
  embeds its own trimmed simulator (readouts, Run/Reset, canvas locked to the
  trial's fixed conditions) driven by one shared script, because Buzz cannot
  open linked/hosted files from a template. Below each simulator is that
  trial's worksheet-style chart.
- The template intentionally contains NO solving directions, formulas, sign
  hints, or conclusions - only setup facts, sims, charts, and questions. The
  numeric prompts are short chart labels ("Total P Before ________ kg*m/s");
  the chart cell provides the trial context, so slot order is critical.
  Teaching lives in the standalone sim's Guide/Hint overlays.
- Chart columns run chronologically: Mass | Initial Velocity | Total
  Momentum BEFORE | Final Velocity | Total Momentum AFTER, with the two
  momentum columns each holding a separate <a:question></a:question> in the
  same chart row - ten numeric questions (1 pt each).
- All chart velocities use magnitude + direction arrows, never +/- signs.
  The yellow Final Velocity cells are interactive scratch widgets (number
  box + two-choice arrow picker, ungraded/unsaved) that students fill from
  the labeled velocity arrows on the balls; they then convert arrows to
  signs for the momentum calculations.
- CRITICAL Buzz setting: assessments default to RANDOMIZED question order,
  which fills the template slots in shuffled order and scrambles the charts.
  Turn OFF randomize/shuffle question order in the assessment settings.
- Four written-answer (essay) questions follow the trials (2 pts each):
  11 momentum before vs after, 12 zero total momentum with moving balls,
  13 total velocity is not conserved, 14 why real colliding objects stop.
  Fourteen questions, 18 points total.
- Every trial has FIXED initial conditions - the mass and speed controls were
  removed, so all students collect identical data. Students use the trials to
  prove conservation of momentum and examine when KE is conserved.
- Numeric grading works because Trial 1 is fixed: m1=1.0 kg moving right at
  3.0 m/s, m2=2.0 kg at rest, e=100% -> v1'=-1.00, v2'=+2.00,
  p=+3.00 kg*m/s and KE=4.50 J before and after.
- The simulator is now a pure sim: the learning-goals card, the fill-in data
  chart card, the lab-evidence card, and the inline directions were removed
  from collison-lab.html; directions and the sign rule live in the Guide and
  Hint overlays, and everything else lives in the Buzz template.

Remaining work before publishing
1. Create the fourteen Buzz questions in slot order (ten numeric: Trial 1
   before, Trial 1 after, ... Trial 5 after; then essays 11-14) and upload
   the template. Do NOT use one-question-per-page; each trial's two questions
   must render together inside that trial's chart.
2. Preview in Buzz and confirm the five embedded simulators draw and run
   (if Buzz strips the template script, host collison-lab.html and add a
   link in the directions card as the fallback).
3. Check grading with the answer key in setup-instructions.html:
   +3.00/+3.00, 0.00/0.00, +7.50/+7.50, +4.25/+4.25, +1.50/+1.50.
4. Optional: add KE before/after questions per trial the same way (each is
   one more <a:question></a:question> cell plus one more Buzz question; keep order):
   Trial 1 KE 4.50 both sides; Trial 2 KE 7.50 both sides;
   Trial 3 KE 13.13 both sides; Trial 4 KE 6.81 -> 3.61;
   Trial 5 KE 11.25 -> 3.15.
5. Optional: add a file-upload evidence question if screenshot evidence is
   wanted, or a KE-focused essay (when is KE conserved / defining
   elasticity) to extend the written analysis.
