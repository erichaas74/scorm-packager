FRICTION FORCE LAB: STATIC VS. KINETIC
UNIT 3 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Become a friction detective! You will pull blocks until they break loose, catch the static-force peak, track the lower sliding force, and use your own completed charts to identify a mystery surface.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Measure maximum static and sliding kinetic friction from force-time graphs.
- Calculate coefficients of friction and separate material effects from normal-force effects.
- Use chart evidence to identify Unknown A and defend a complete friction conclusion.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Run the four known materials and complete the Part 1 chart from the graph readings.
2. Run Wood with +0 kg, +5 kg, and +10 kg and complete the Part 2 calculation chart.
3. Test Unknown A and calculate its coefficient pair.
4. Answer Questions 1-10 from the completed charts and force-time graphs.
5. Use specific values from all three parts in Questions 11-12.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 12 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- This lesson uses 10 automatic points because students complete a provided data or measurement chart.
- No file upload earns or replaces points.
