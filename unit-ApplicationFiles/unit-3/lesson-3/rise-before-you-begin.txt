UNIT 3 • LESSON 3 — BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Friction Force Lab: Static vs. Kinetic

Read these directions before selecting Begin. This activity is your opportunity to practice the lesson concepts and collect the evidence you will need for the assessment.

YOUR GOAL
Compare static and kinetic friction and identify the maximum static-friction force.

BE PREPARED
Have your lesson notes available. Use a calculator and scratch paper when the investigation includes calculations or data comparisons.

WHAT TO DO
1. Increase the applied force gradually and observe when motion begins.
2. Compare force readings before and after the object starts moving.
3. Use the graph or data to distinguish static from kinetic friction.
4. Complete the assessment using evidence and submit.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Complete every required investigation step and all required assessment questions. Review unanswered items before submitting. If the lab shows a Submit / Resubmit button, select it and wait for confirmation before returning to Rise.

When you understand the directions, select Begin.
