Buzz Setup: Unit 3 Lesson 3 Friction Force Lab

What this activity is

This is a traditional science lab report divided into three distinct investigation sections. The self-contained template u3l3-friction-force-buzz-assessment-template.html places the original simulation surface first, then a fixed Unknown A setup, then a fixed Wood added-mass setup, followed by the 13 Buzz questions.

Part 1: Students use four material choices (Wood, Rubber, Plastic, and Sandpaper) to find four pairs of friction coefficients. The extra-mass and constant-force controls are removed from this section.

Part 2: Students use Wood with exactly three added-mass choices: +0 kg, +5 kg, and +10 kg. They read each graph and complete the three-row calculation chart.

Part 3: Students use Unknown A only. They read the graph and answer the mystery-material questions. There is no student-filled chart in this section.

The template preserves the standalone lab's spring scale, force-time graph, block rendering, and velocity meter. It does not print the answer-key friction values in a readout; students must read the peak and sliding plateaus from each graph and use their collected charts as evidence.

Step 1: Create the Buzz assessment

  1. Use the title Unit 3 Lesson 3: Friction Force Lab - Static vs Kinetic.
  2. Paste the complete contents of buzz-assessment-questions.txt into Buzz's Text editor / Edit all questions screen.
  3. Confirm that Buzz creates 13 questions worth 40 total points: 12 BusyBee-graded essay questions and one file upload question.
  4. Use busybee-rubric-metadata.json as the rubric metadata for the written responses.

Turn OFF randomize/shuffle question order. Do not use one-question-per-page. The template fills its 13 placeholders in display order, and all report questions must remain together at the end of the lab.
