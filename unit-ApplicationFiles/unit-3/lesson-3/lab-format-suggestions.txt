Lab Format Notes: Unit 3 Lesson 3 - Friction Force Lab

Current lab format
- Traditional science lab flow: students run trials, complete a data chart, calculate coefficients, and answer a full lab write-up in Buzz.
- The simulation includes a Lab Data Chart with required rows for Wood, Rubber, Plastic, Sandpaper, Wood +5 kg, and Unknown A.
- Students enter graph-read values for peak static force and sliding kinetic force. The chart calculates mu_s and mu_k from the student's entries.
- Extra mass is shown as a gray block on top of the material block. Normal force and weight vectors are hidden except in the guide or hint view.
- The Buzz question list now covers title/question, purpose/background, hypothesis, variables, materials/procedure, data chart, calculations, added-mass comparison, constant-force observations, graph evidence, analysis, error/improvement, and conclusion.
- Suggested total is 40 points. All question scores are integers.

Teacher/BusyBee grading notes
- Grade the data chart for completeness and reasonable graph-reading variation, not exact perfection.
- Required chart columns: material, extra mass, total mass, normal force, peak static force, sliding kinetic force, mu_s, and mu_k.
- Expected chart values are included in Question 6 of buzz-assessment-questions.txt.
- Written questions include Always feedback and rubric text to support BusyBee grading.
- Human review is recommended if the pasted chart conflicts strongly with the student's graph upload or if the chart is missing.

Future optional improvements
