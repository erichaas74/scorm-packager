Lab Format Notes: Unit 3 Lesson 3 - Friction Force Lab

Current lab format
- Buzz-native traditional science lab flow: students run trials, complete a data chart, calculate coefficients, and answer a full lab write-up in Buzz.
- Conversion complete: upload `u3l3-friction-force-buzz-assessment-template.html`, use `setup-instructions.html`, and preview with `u3l3-friction-force-buzz-assessment-template-preview.html`.
- Part 1 uses the original spring-scale and force-time graph interface with four known material rows: Wood, Rubber, Plastic, and Sandpaper. Unnecessary extra-mass and constant-force controls are hidden.
- Part 2 uses a fixed Unknown A spring-scale and graph setup. Students enter the normal force, graph readings, and calculated coefficients.
- Part 3 uses a fixed Wood spring-scale and graph setup with +0, +2, +5, and +10 kg rows to test whether added mass changes the coefficients.
- Students enter graph-read values for peak static force and sliding kinetic force. Each focused chart calculates mu_s and mu_k from the student's entries.
- The Buzz question list now covers title/question, purpose/background, hypothesis, variables, materials/procedure, three-part data evidence, calculations, Unknown A, added-mass comparison, graph evidence, analysis, error/improvement, and conclusion.
- Suggested total is 40 points. All question scores are integers.

Teacher/BusyBee grading notes
- Grade the data chart for completeness and reasonable graph-reading variation, not exact perfection.
- Required evidence: four known-material rows in Part 1, an Unknown A row with student-provided normal force in Part 2, and four Wood added-mass rows in Part 3. Each row includes normal force, peak static force, sliding kinetic force, mu_s, and mu_k.
- Expected chart values are included in Question 6 of buzz-assessment-questions.txt.
- Written questions include Always feedback and rubric text to support BusyBee grading.
- Human review is recommended if the pasted chart conflicts strongly with the student's graph upload or if the chart is missing.

Future optional improvements
