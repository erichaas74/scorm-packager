Ready. Run the gold Required Assessment Jump to draw the graph used in the questions below.
Step 1

Read the Push Time

Find where the net force leaves 0 N and where it returns to 0 N.

Step 2

Read the Peak Force

Use the y-axis scale to read the highest point of the triangular force pulse.

Step 3

Find the Average Force

For this triangle, the average force during the push is half of the peak force.

Step 4

Determine the Impulse

Use the area under the graph. You can use the triangle area formula or count grid boxes.

Step 5

Connect to Momentum

The impulse-momentum theorem says the impulse equals the jumper's change in momentum.

Step 6

Find Takeoff Velocity

The jumper starts from rest on the force plate, so the vertical takeoff velocity is found from J = m * change in v.

Step 7

Find Maximum Height

Use the takeoff velocity as the initial vertical velocity of the jump after the feet leave the ground.
