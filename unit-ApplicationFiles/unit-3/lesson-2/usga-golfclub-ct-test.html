Your Testing Assignment

  1. Part A — Calibration: Pick a face material on the tester and press Drop Pendulum. Test all five known materials. The machine only plots the raw force–time trace — it does not compute results for you. Hover over the graph to read time and force values, calculate each CT yourself, and record your readings in the Test Log notebook below. The tester re-arms itself a few seconds after each drop.

Test it, measure its trace, and use your Part A data to decide how stiff its face is and whether it conforms to the USGA limit (CT ≤ 257 µs).
