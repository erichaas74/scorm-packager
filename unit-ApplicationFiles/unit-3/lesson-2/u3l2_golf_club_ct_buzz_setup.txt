USGA GOLF CLUB CT COMPLIANCE TEST
UNIT 3 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Step into the equipment lab as a golf-club compliance engineer! You will fire a pendulum tester, read razor-thin force-time pulses, and decide whether a prototype passes the official CT limit.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Read contact time and peak force from fixed force-time traces.
- Relate Newton's Third Law force pairs to different accelerations for different masses.
- Use a completed compliance chart and the 257-microsecond limit to justify an engineering ruling.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Answer the two Newton's Third Law checks before using the tester.
2. Test Soft Polymer, Alloy, Thin Titanium, and Thick Titanium and complete Questions 3-8 in the provided chart.
3. Test Rigid Steel and Prototype X-7 and complete Questions 9-10.
4. Use at least three chart entries to explain the calibration pattern in Question 11.
5. File the final compliance ruling with both CT measurements in Question 12.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 12 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- This lesson uses 10 automatic points because students complete a provided data or measurement chart.
- No file upload earns or replaces points.
