Lab Format Suggestions: Unit 3 Lesson 2 - USGA Golf Club CT Compliance Test

Buzz-native conversion status: complete.

- The assessment uses `u3l2-golf-club-ct-buzz-assessment-template.html`, a self-contained fixed-mode pendulum tester with all six force-time traces.
- `Prototype X-7` is locked to the original model's Profile B values (closest to Alloy, CT 266 microseconds), so every simulation reading is suitable for native Buzz grading.
- A four-question Newton's Third Law pre-lab uses a club force-time reference graph to assess the equal-and-opposite ball force, the lighter ball's greater acceleration, and an evidence-based collision conclusion.
- Part 1 presents three four-material rows in this order: contact time, peak force, and average force. Its three follow-up questions assess greatest average acceleration, longest acceleration time, and speed increase from force-time area (impulse).
- The 26 native questions collect contact time, peak force, and average force for all six faces, then assess Part 1 force-time reasoning, known-face compliance, and the prototype ruling.
- Contact-time and average-force answer bands are intentionally wide because students estimate them visually from the graph. Peak-force bands are narrower because the 5 kN grid lines make that reading more precise.
- Do not shuffle questions or use one-question-per-page: the template's 26 slots must stay in display order.
