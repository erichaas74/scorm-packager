IMPULSE JUMP LAB
UNIT 3 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Turn a jump into a chain of physics evidence! You will measure the area under a force-time pulse, convert it to momentum and takeoff speed, and predict how high the athlete rises.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Read peak force and contact time and calculate impulse from graph area.
- Apply the impulse-momentum theorem to find takeoff velocity and jump height.
- Compare graph shape, force, time, impulse, and performance across jump profiles.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Run the gold Required Assessment Jump to draw the fixed triangular graph.
2. Answer Questions 1-8 through the calculation ladder from contact time to height.
3. Run at least one named practice profile and compare its graph with the required jump.
4. Show the full fixed calculation chain in Question 9.
5. Use evidence from both graphs in Question 10.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 10 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 8 auto-graded points plus 7 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 3 points and 4 points.
- This lesson does not use the 10-automatic-point chart model because students do not fill a provided chart.
- No file upload earns or replaces points.

- All 10 questions render and the standalone animator dependency resolves.
