Buzz Assessment Questions: Unit 1 Lesson 1 Additions - Alternate Motion Graphs Build

Assessment setup
- Student opens the hosted lesson from the Buzz directions.
- Note: this folder currently appears to be an alternate Motion Graphs / Mail Carrier Mystery build, even though the Unit 1 branch page labels the card as City Blocks Challenge.
- Use these questions only if this folder remains a Motion Graphs activity.
- Suggested total: 15 points. Every question uses a whole-number point value.

Question 1: Completion Evidence
Type: File upload
Points: 2
Prompt: Upload a screenshot showing the completed assessment or a completed graph-reading case from this lesson.
Grading guide: Full credit if the screenshot clearly shows the graph, prompt, and student result.

Question 2: Stop or Pause Evidence
Type: Short answer
Points: 2
Prompt: Record one time interval where the truck stopped or paused. How can you tell from the graph?
Grading guide: Full credit identifies a correct interval and explains the flat position graph or zero velocity.

Question 3: Direction Change
Type: Short answer
Points: 2
Prompt: Record one time when the truck changed direction. What graph feature showed the change?
Grading guide: Full credit identifies a change in slope or a velocity sign change.

Question 4: Position Reading
Type: Short answer
Points: 2
Prompt: Record one position value from the position-time graph, including time and position.
Grading guide: Full credit includes a valid time, position, and units.

Question 5: Velocity Reading
Type: Short answer
Points: 1
Prompt: Record one velocity value from the velocity-time graph, including the interval and velocity.
Grading guide: Full credit includes a valid interval, velocity, and units.

Question 6: Distance and Displacement
Type: Essay
Points: 2
Prompt: Using one case from the lesson, explain how the total distance traveled compares with the displacement.
Grading guide: Full credit correctly distinguishes path length from change in position.

Question 7: Average Velocity
Type: Essay or short answer
Points: 1
Prompt: Show how to calculate average velocity for one trip using displacement divided by total time.
Grading guide: Full credit includes displacement, time, calculation, and units.

Question 8: Graph Match Reasoning
Type: Essay
Points: 2
Prompt: Explain how you matched a motion graph to the truck's motion. Use at least two graph clues.
Grading guide: Full credit uses clues such as starting position, final position, slope, pauses, direction changes, or velocity sign.

Question 9: Error Check
Type: Essay
Points: 1
Prompt: What is one mistake a student might make when reading these graphs, and how can they avoid it?
Grading guide: Full credit names a plausible graph-reading mistake and gives a prevention strategy.
