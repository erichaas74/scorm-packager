REACTION TIME KINEMATICS LAB
UNIT 1 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Put your reflexes on the clock, then watch a split-second delay grow into a race-deciding gap! You will connect ruler drops, drag racing, and sprint starts with constant-acceleration and constant-speed models.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Calculate reaction time from a falling-ruler distance.
- Predict how reaction delay affects an accelerating drag race and a constant-speed sprint.
- Compare visual and auditory trial evidence using human-response limits.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Record multiple clean falling-ruler trials in Level 1.
2. Complete drag-race trials on the canonical 300 m strip in Level 2.
3. Complete auditory sprint trials in Level 3 and note false-start behavior.
4. Answer Questions 1-8 using the fixed calculations and concepts.
5. Use evidence from your visual, drag-race, and sprint trials in Questions 9-10.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 10 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 8 auto-graded points plus 7 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 3 points and 4 points.
- This lesson does not use the 10-automatic-point chart model; its live evidence is evaluated in the final BusyBee responses.
- No file upload earns or replaces points.
