Lab Format Status: Unit 1 Lesson 1 - Motion Graphs Lab

Buzz delivery format
- Complete: SCORM 1.2 application package.
- The lab owns its randomized assessment, retry logic, progress display, and score.
- Do not add native Buzz assessment questions or a Buzz assessment template.

Included student experience
- 13 selectable practice cases.
- 15 required randomized assessment questions.
- 5 optional bonus questions.
- Position-time and velocity-time graph reading, graph matching, and motion analysis.
- Best-attempt scoring with a maximum reported score of 15 points.

Verified LMS contract
- cmi.core.score.raw: earned points, capped at 15.
- cmi.core.score.min: 0.
- cmi.core.score.max: 15.
- cmi.core.lesson_status: incomplete until all 15 core questions are attempted, then completed.
- cmi.suspend_data: compact attempted/correct state for all answered questions.
- cmi.core.lesson_location: assessment or complete.

Publishing checklist
1. Upload unit-1/lesson-1.zip as a SCORM 1.2 activity.
2. Set the Buzz gradebook value to 15 points.
3. Allow relaunch/resubmission if students may improve their scores.
4. Preview as a student and verify that Practice and Assessment both render.
5. Attempt all 15 core questions and confirm Buzz changes the activity to completed.
6. Confirm Buzz shows the raw score on a 0-15 scale.
7. Relaunch once and confirm the question status indicators are restored.
