6) A mail truck moves from x = 10 m to x = 34 m in 6 s. What is its average velocity in m/s?
When answer is correct Correct. Average velocity is displacement divided by elapsed time: (34 - 10) / 6 = 4 m/s.
When answer is incorrect First subtract the initial position from the final position, then divide by 6 s.
A

3.9..4.1
