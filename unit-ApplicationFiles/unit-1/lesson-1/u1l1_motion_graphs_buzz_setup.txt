MOTION GRAPHS: MAIL CARRIER MYSTERY
UNIT 1 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Crack a mail-route mystery by making a moving truck and two motion graphs tell the same story! You will follow stops, reversals, slopes, and velocity signs until you can read motion from a graph at a glance.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Connect position-time slope to the magnitude and direction of velocity.
- Recognize stops, constant velocity, speeding up, and direction changes on paired graphs.
- Use evidence from two fixed explorer cases to defend a motion interpretation.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Open each of the three fixed cases in the Motion Graph Explorer.
2. Play or scrub each case and compare the truck, position-time graph, and velocity-time graph.
3. Answer Questions 1-8 using the fixed graph relationships and calculations.
4. Use one named case as evidence in Question 9.
5. Compare two named cases and both graph types in Question 10.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 10 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 8 auto-graded points plus 7 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 3 points and 4 points.
- This lesson does not use the 10-automatic-point chart model; its live evidence is evaluated in the final BusyBee responses.
- No file upload earns or replaces points.
