MOTION GRAPHS: MAIL CARRIER MYSTERY
UNIT 1 BUZZ APPLICATION ASSIGNMENT GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Crack a mail-route mystery by making a moving truck and two motion graphs tell the same story! You will follow stops, reversals, slopes, and velocity signs until you can read motion from a graph at a glance.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Connect position-time slope to the magnitude and direction of velocity.
- Recognize stops, constant velocity, speeding up, and direction changes on paired graphs.
- Complete the 15 required application questions using motion evidence.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE ASSIGNMENT
1. Use Explore Cases to investigate the truck, position-time graph, and velocity-time graph.
2. Open Assignment Questions and complete required Questions 1-15.
3. Use the feedback to review each answer; correctness does not change completion credit.
4. Questions 16-20 are optional and do not affect assignment completion.
5. Confirm the completion display reads 15 / 15 required.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assignment is worth 15 completion points: one point for each attempted required question, Questions 1-15.
- Correctness provides instructional feedback but does not change completion credit.
- Questions 16-20 are optional and do not add points.
- No native Buzz questions, BusyBee responses, or file uploads are used.
