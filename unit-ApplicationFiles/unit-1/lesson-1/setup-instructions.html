Buzz Setup: Unit 1 Lesson 1 Motion Graphs Lab

What this activity is

This is a self-contained SCORM 1.2 application. The simulation creates randomized graph-reading questions, grades answers, preserves best attempts, and sends one score to Buzz. It includes 15 required core questions and 5 optional bonus questions.

Do not create native Buzz questions for this lesson. The assessment is already part of the application.

Upload and configure

  1. Create a SCORM activity titled Unit 1 Lesson 1: Motion Graphs Lab - Mail Carrier Mystery.
  2. Upload lesson-1.zip.
  3. Choose SCORM 1.2 if Buzz asks for a package version.
  4. Set the gradebook points possible to 15 and use the SCORM score as the grade source.
  5. Allow relaunch/resubmission if students may retry questions to improve their best score.

Score and completion contract

SCORM valueApplication behavior
cmi.core.score.rawCorrect core plus bonus points, capped at 15
cmi.core.score.min0
cmi.core.score.max15
cmi.core.lesson_statusIncomplete until all 15 core questions are attempted; completed afterward

Student preview checklist

  • Confirm the title reads “Motion Graphs Lab - Mail Carrier Mystery.”
  • Start the lab and confirm all 13 practice cases appear.
  • Switch to Assessment and confirm 15 core plus 5 bonus questions appear.
  • Submit an answer and confirm Buzz receives a score on the 0-15 scale.
  • Attempt all 15 core questions and confirm the activity becomes completed.
  • Close and relaunch the activity; confirm answered/retry-needed indicators return.
