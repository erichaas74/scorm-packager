Question 1: Interval Distance Trend

Type: Essay

Points: 2

Prompt: Describe the trend in the interval distance column. What does that trend tell you about the object's speed?

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit Explains that interval distances increase over equal time intervals and connects that increase to the object moving faster as it falls.
Partial credit Identifies that the distances increase but does not clearly connect the trend to speed, or gives a correct speed explanation with weak data reference.
No credit Says the motion is constant speed, describes the wrong column, or gives no usable explanation.

Question 2: Evidence of Acceleration

Type: Essay

Points: 2

Prompt: Explain how you can tell from the data that the object was accelerating during the fall.

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit States that acceleration means velocity changes, then uses the increasing interval distances or increasing speed over equal time intervals as evidence.
Partial credit Correctly says the object speeds up or accelerates but gives limited or vague evidence from the table.
No credit Confuses acceleration with total distance only, claims the speed stayed the same, or does not use data evidence.

Question 3: Interval Ratio Pattern

Type: Essay

Points: 1

Prompt: Compare your interval ratio column to Galileo's odd-number pattern: 1, 3, 5, 7, and so on.

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit Recognizes that the interval ratios should be close to odd numbers and explains that this is evidence of constant acceleration.
Partial credit Mentions the odd-number pattern but gives little explanation, or notices approximate agreement without naming the pattern clearly.
No credit Compares to the wrong pattern, says the ratios should all be equal, or gives no pattern-based answer.

Question 4: Total Distance Ratio Pattern

Type: Essay

Points: 1

Prompt: Compare your total distance ratio column to the square-number pattern: 1, 4, 9, 16, and so on.

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit Recognizes that total distance ratios should be close to square numbers and connects this to distance growing with time squared.
Partial credit Mentions the square-number pattern but gives little explanation, or identifies the pattern with weak connection to the data.
No credit Compares to the wrong pattern, says the total distance increases linearly, or gives no useful comparison.

Question 5: Sources of Error

Type: Essay

Points: 2

Prompt: Identify at least one source of error in the digital experiment and explain how it could affect the data.

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit Names a plausible error source and explains its effect on measurement or interpretation. Examples include reading the ruler imprecisely, rounding, timing/scrubbing errors, screen resolution, or recording the wrong row.
Partial credit Names a plausible error source but gives little explanation of how it affects the data.
No credit Gives an unrelated error source or says there could be no error without explanation.

Question 6: Galileo's Tools

Type: Essay

Points: 2

Prompt: Explain why Galileo's water clocks, bells, or inclined planes could introduce more uncertainty than a modern digital simulation or video measurement.

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit Explains that Galileo's tools had limited precision and human timing/measurement uncertainty, then compares that to the more precise timing and repeatability of digital tools.
Partial credit Mentions that older tools were less accurate but gives limited comparison or explanation.
No credit Does not connect the tools to uncertainty, precision, timing, or measurement error.

Question 7: Completed Chart Evidence

Type: File Upload

Points: 2

Prompt: Upload your completed Galileo data chart from the embedded lab above. Use the Download Evidence Image button or upload a clear screenshot showing all time rows and all four calculated columns.

BusyBee grading guide

Full credit Upload is readable and shows all time rows with distance dropped, interval distance, interval ratio, and total distance ratio completed.
Partial credit Upload is present but partially cut off, difficult to read, or missing one column or several rows.
No credit No upload is provided, the wrong file is uploaded, or the evidence does not show the completed chart.
