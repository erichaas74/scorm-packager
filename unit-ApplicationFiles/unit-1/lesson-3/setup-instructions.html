Unit 1 Lesson 3 Buzz Setup

Step 1: Create the Buzz questions

  1. Create a Buzz Assessment titled Unit 1 Lesson 3: Galileo Acceleration Lab Analysis.
  2. Paste the full contents of buzz-assessment-questions.txt into the Buzz Assessment text editor or Edit all questions screen.
  3. The question bank contains six Essay questions and one File Upload question, worth 12 points total.
  4. Keep the questions in the exact order shown. Each <a:question></a:question> slot pulls in the next Buzz question.

Step 2: Upload and preview the self-contained template

  1. Upload u1l3-galileo-buzz-assessment-template.html as the assessment template in Buzz.
  2. Turn off randomize or shuffle question order.
  3. Do not use one-question-per-page for this assessment.
  4. Preview as a student and confirm the page title says Galileo Acceleration Lab Assessment.
  5. If the preview says Collision Lab, the Unit 3 Lesson 4 template was uploaded by mistake. Delete that template upload and upload u1l3-galileo-buzz-assessment-template.html from this folder.
  6. Confirm the embedded lab, seven Buzz question slots, and evidence upload all appear.
  7. Complete the table in the preview and confirm the completed chart plus Download Evidence Image button appear.
  8. No hosted lab URL or external lab files are needed for this Lesson 3 assessment template.
