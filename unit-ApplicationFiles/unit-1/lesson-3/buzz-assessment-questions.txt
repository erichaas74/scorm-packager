9) Part 3 of 3 - Average acceleration. Use the speeds you calculated in Parts 1 and 2. Here is the motion equation: v_last = v_first + a * delta_t. Rearranged: a = (v_last - v_first) / delta_t, where a is acceleration and delta_t is the time between the speeds. For constant acceleration, an interval's average speed equals its speed at the midpoint. Our midpoint times are 0.05 s and 0.65 s, so delta_t = 0.60 s. Subtract the first speed from the last, then divide by 0.60 s. Enter the acceleration in m/s^2, taking downward as positive.
