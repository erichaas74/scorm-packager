CITY BLOCKS CHALLENGE
UNIT 1 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Navigate a city like a motion-planning expert! You will build routes that hit exact distance, displacement, speed, and velocity targets—and discover why waiting can be part of a winning physics strategy.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Distinguish route distance from start-to-finish displacement.
- Calculate average speed and average velocity from route evidence.
- Explain how backtracking and waiting change motion quantities differently.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Complete all six City Blocks levels and watch both target columns.
2. Use WAIT when a rate target requires more elapsed time without more motion.
3. Answer Questions 1-8 using the fixed level targets.
4. Use one completed route calculation in Question 9.
5. Compare a displacement level with a rate level in Question 10.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 10 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

ANSWER CHECKLIST
- Record the level, distance, displacement, and elapsed time before resetting the route.
- Time in this simulation is in minutes. Label each calculated rate as average speed or average velocity, in blocks per minute.
- For the strategy comparison, use two completed levels and explain separately what backtracking changes and what WAIT changes.

- This lesson does not use the 10-automatic-point chart model; its live evidence is evaluated in the final BusyBee responses.
