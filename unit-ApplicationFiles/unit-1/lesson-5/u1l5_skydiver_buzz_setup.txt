SKYDIVER GRAPHS AND FORMATION DIVE
UNIT 1 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Dive into motion graphs from a skydiver’s point of view! You will chase terminal velocity, change drag with body position, hold formation, and explain why a falling partner can appear to rise.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Interpret velocity-time slope, constant velocity, and acceleration-time sign.
- Connect body position and parachute deployment to drag and terminal velocity.
- Explain formation changes using ground-frame and relative-motion evidence.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Play the fixed scripted jump and inspect both graph types.
2. Complete a formation dive using Dive, Neutral, and Spread positions.
3. Deploy the parachute and observe the velocity, acceleration, and relative-altitude graphs.
4. Answer Questions 1-8 using the fixed graph relationships.
5. Use specific evidence from your own formation dive in Questions 9-10.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 10 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

ANSWER CHECKLIST
- Record one body-position change and what happened before and after it. A clear graph description counts as evidence; exact readings are optional.
- For your strategy, cover body position and drag, parachute timing relative to your partner, a feature of each graph, and apparent upward motion.
- Downward is positive in these graphs. Distinguish the direction of acceleration from the direction the diver is moving.
