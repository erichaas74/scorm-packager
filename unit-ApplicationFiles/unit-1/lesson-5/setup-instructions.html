Unit 1 Lesson 5 Buzz Setup

Step 1: Create the Buzz questions

  1. Create a Buzz Assessment titled Unit 1 Lesson 5: Skydiver Graphs and Challenge.
  2. Paste the full contents of buzz-assessment-questions.txt into the Buzz question text editor.
  3. The question bank contains eight fixed graph and motion questions worth 15 points total. Every question uses a whole-number point value.
  4. Keep question order locked. The template fills the seven question slots in order.

Step 2: Upload the self-contained template

  1. Upload u1l5-skydiver-buzz-assessment-template.html as the assessment template.
  2. Turn off randomized question order.
  3. Do not use one-question-per-page.
  4. Keep busybee-rubric-metadata.json with the lesson materials if BusyBee will use external rubric metadata.
  5. Preview as a student and confirm the title says Skydiver Graphs and Challenge Buzz Assessment.
  6. Play the scripted jump and confirm the five graph questions appear after Part 1.
  7. Complete the challenge jump and confirm the evidence upload and strategy questions appear after Part 2.

Grading note

Part 1 uses fixed answers because every student sees the same scripted graph. Part 2 is student-dependent, so grade the challenge with the uploaded evidence image, the student's explanation, and busybee-rubric-metadata.json instead of fixed numeric answers.
