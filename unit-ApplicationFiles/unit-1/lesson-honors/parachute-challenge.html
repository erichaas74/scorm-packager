Flight conditions

Starts from rest at the pad. Upward is positive.

  • Powered flight: net acceleration +15 m/s².
  • Fuel lasts 4.00 s; the engine then cuts off automatically and cannot restart. Maximum possible altitude is 300 m.
  • Engine off, chute closed: acceleration −10 m/s²; air resistance is neglected.
  • The parachute can open while descending at 5 m/s or faster.
  • Idealized parachute: net acceleration +12 m/s² until velocity reaches −5 m/s, then a constant −5 m/s descent. The chute stays open.
