MISSION CONTROL RETRO-BURN LANDING
UNIT 1 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Reconstruct a rocket launch in Sections 1–4, ending with retro-burn displacement and duration. In Section 5, control engine cutoff and parachute deployment, try to land safely, and calculate airborne time from your flight record.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Apply constant-acceleration equations across connected flight phases.
- Calculate apex height, flight time, total distance, and touchdown behavior.
- Distinguish velocity, acceleration, distance, and displacement in a complete mission.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Run the fixed powered-burn section and calculate its displacement and cutoff velocity.
2. Run the coast and apex sections and calculate the added height and time.
3. In Section 4, calculate only the retro-burn displacement and duration, using the original mission.
4. Answer Questions 1-8 using the fixed 20 m/s², 2.5 s mission.
5. In Section 5, launch and choose when to cut the engine; fuel forces cutoff at 4 s. Deploy the parachute during descent and try to land at 6 m/s or less.
6. Calculate flight times, percentage, and landing safety in the app, then select Submit Section 5. All answers must be correct to complete the 4-point section.
7. Submit the Buzz assessment after completing your work. Incomplete Section 5 work leaves its hidden response blank.
8. Before submitting, answer Questions 1-8 and confirm Section 5 reports complete. The hidden ninth answer is managed by the app.

ANSWER CHECKLIST
- For Questions 1-8, use the fixed mission values shown in the application, including coast acceleration of -10 m/s^2. Do not substitute 9.8.
- Keep upward positive and separate distance traveled from signed displacement. Check which phase each question asks about.
- Section 5 uses its own recorded parachute flight. Its completion evidence is generated by the application; do not type or invent that evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 11 auto-graded points plus 4 BusyBee points.
- The final 1 evidence response(s) are worth 4 points.
- Section 5 is binary: complete evidence earns 4 points; blank, missing, or invalid evidence is incomplete and earns 0. No partial credit.
- No file upload earns or replaces points.

- BusyBee scoring: Award points only in 0.5-point increments, including whole points, from 0 to the question maximum. Select a rubric-supported allowed score; never report hundredths such as 2.85/3. Do not average or percentage-scale rubric criteria into an off-grid score. A binary completion rubric still permits only 0 or full credit.

REVIEW-BASED GRADING GUIDANCE
Grade the understanding shown. Accept equivalent wording, reasonable rounding and simulation precision; do not demand repeated units already supplied by a blank or an explanation that the blank did not request. For written responses, credit correct concepts, methods and consistent follow-through. Deduct for the original error without repeatedly penalizing its correctly carried-forward consequences. A minor omission is not a wholly incorrect answer. Judge only requested evidence; a clear qualitative graph change is valid when the prompt allows it. Preserve deductions for materially wrong values, misconceptions and missing required work. Binary completion items remain binary. Check the published assignment version and its actual question numbers before grading.
Begin the overall comment with varied, brief encouragement naming only skills demonstrated in this submission. Then address only missed items, using the actual Question N label and one short correction per item, at most 25 words excluding the label. End with a freshly written positive sentence specific to this student's work; do not recycle a standard closing or use "Keep up the effort." Do not include scores, percentages, points earned or deducted, internal IDs, or em dashes. Question numbers and physics values are allowed. Never invent evidence, a mistake, or mastery. If every answer is correct, give only the evidence-based opening and specific closing. Per-question fields contain only the relevant correction; do not repeat the opening and closing in each field.
