MISSION CONTROL RETRO-BURN LANDING
UNIT 1 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Take the flight engineer’s chair and reconstruct a four-phase rocket mission! You will calculate the powered burn, coast to apex, free fall, and retro burn before explaining how the velocity and acceleration story fits together.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Apply constant-acceleration equations across connected flight phases.
- Calculate apex height, flight time, total distance, and touchdown behavior.
- Distinguish velocity, acceleration, distance, and displacement in a complete mission.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Run the fixed powered-burn section and calculate its displacement and cutoff velocity.
2. Run the coast and apex sections and calculate the added height and time.
3. Run the complete mission with readouts hidden.
4. Answer Questions 1-8 using the fixed 20 m/s², 2.5 s mission.
5. Use the full path and all four phases in Questions 9-10.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 10 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 8 auto-graded points plus 7 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 3 points and 4 points.
- This lesson does not use the 10-automatic-point chart model; its live evidence is evaluated in the final BusyBee responses.
- No file upload earns or replaces points.
