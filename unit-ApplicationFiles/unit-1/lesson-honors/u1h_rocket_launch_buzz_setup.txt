MISSION CONTROL RETRO-BURN LANDING
UNIT 1 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Reconstruct the fixed rocket launch in Sections 1?4, then calculate the acceleration needed after a late retro burn and explain the four phases of motion in Section 5.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Apply constant-acceleration equations across connected flight phases.
- Calculate apex height, flight time, total distance, and touchdown behavior.
- Distinguish velocity, acceleration, distance, and displacement in a complete mission.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Run Sections 1-4 and answer Questions 1-8 using the fixed +20 m/s^2, 2.5 s powered burn and -10 m/s^2 gravity.
2. In Section 5, explain how to recover from a late retro burn and calculate the required net acceleration in Question 9.
3. Analyze velocity and acceleration in all four phases in Question 10, using at least two calculated values.
4. Enter your written answers in the visible Buzz response boxes, then submit the assessment.
5. Before submitting, answer Questions 1-8 and write your explanations for Questions 9-10.

ANSWER CHECKLIST
- For Questions 1-8, use the fixed mission values shown in the application, including coast acceleration of -10 m/s^2. Do not substitute 9.8.
- Keep upward positive and separate distance traveled from signed displacement. Check which phase each question asks about.
- For Section 5, write your own explanations about a late retro burn and all four flight phases. Use the new givens in Question 9; use calculated fixed-mission values as evidence in Question 10.
