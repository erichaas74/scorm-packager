Buzz and Netlify Setup

Step 1: Upload this folder to Netlify

  1. Upload the entire lesson-3-netlify-galileo-upload folder to Netlify.
  2. After deploy, open the Netlify site and confirm the lab works at the site root, such as https://galileo-drop-lab.netlify.app/.
  3. Copy the Netlify site URL, such as https://your-site-name.netlify.app.

Step 2: Prepare the Buzz template

  1. Open u1l3-galileo-netlify-buzz-assessment-template.html.
  2. Confirm both lab links in u1l3-galileo-netlify-buzz-assessment-template.html use your real Netlify site URL.
  3. Save the file.

Step 3: Create the Buzz assessment questions

  1. Create a Buzz Assessment titled Galileo Acceleration Lab Analysis.
  2. Create the six Essay questions listed in buzz-assessment-questions.html.
  3. Add Question 7 as a screenshot upload question for the completed chart.
  4. Keep them in the exact order shown. The first <a:question></a:question> tag pulls in Question 1, the second pulls in Question 2, and so on.

Step 4: Upload the Buzz template

  1. In the Buzz Assessment editor, upload u1l3-galileo-netlify-buzz-assessment-template.html as the assessment template.
  2. Save the assessment.
  3. Preview as a student and confirm the Galileo lab appears above the native Buzz Essay questions.
