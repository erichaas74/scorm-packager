  only quantities they do not show. No Buzz question asks the student to calculate GR, SR,

- No "Copy values for Buzz" button. The integrity guard blocks paste, so it would be dead
  weight. The trial values stay readable in the data table and the evidence PNG.
- The evidence PNG reproduces the trial-data table but not the graph. Question 2 asks the
  student to read the zero crossing off the on-screen graph, so baking the graph into the
  evidence image would hand over the answer in a downloadable file.

Grading design:
- Evidence based for the student's own trials, which vary because students choose their own
  altitudes and speeds. Those are graded through the upload (Q1) and the Q8 essay, never by
  a fixed numeric key.
- Q2-Q4 grade derived quantities that are the same for every student: the cancellation
  radius read off the graph, the position error from drift times c, and the Lorentz factor.
- Q5-Q7 are fixed conceptual multiple-choice items tied to the GPS preset, the ISS preset,
  and the Hafele-Keating evidence card.
- Q1 is the evidence upload; Q8-Q9 use BusyBee-graded rubrics (style B). Q9 lets the student
  choose any of the six evidence cards, so the rubric grades the reasoning, not the choice.
