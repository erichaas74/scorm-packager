Frequency and resonant length

Lower frequencies have longer wavelengths. For one fork, the 3rd and 5th resonance lengths are three and five times the 1st.

What resonance changes

Resonance makes the sound more intense and louder. The tuning fork sets the frequency, so its pitch does not change as the pipe moves.
