SPECTRAL SHIFT INVESTIGATION
UNIT 5 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Read motion written in starlight! You will match elemental fingerprints, slide spectral lines into alignment, and use wavelength shifts to decide whether distant objects are racing toward us or away from us.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Identify an element from its unique spectral-line pattern.
- Distinguish redshift from blueshift and connect each to radial motion.
- Calculate fractional wavelength shift and approximate radial speed from measured data.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Drag a reference spectrum into the comparison area and identify the matching elemental fingerprint.
2. Move the dashed spectral markers until the observed and reference lines align.
3. Record the measured wavelength shift for all three objects and note each direction of motion.
4. Answer the Buzz questions in order, calculating from the displayed rest wavelength and measured shift rather than a displayed z value.
5. Download the evidence image only if you want a personal record; it is not submitted or graded.
6. Before submitting, confirm that you answered all 8 questions and that the final two responses cite your recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- Evidence downloads are optional records. No file upload earns or replaces points.

TEACHER BUZZ SETUP

3. Confirm the import creates 8 questions worth 15 points: four numeric, two multiple-choice, and two essay questions.
4. Upload u5l5-spectral-shift-buzz-assessment-template.html from this folder as the assessment template.
5. Turn question randomization/shuffling OFF and one-question-per-page OFF.
6. Confirm the template contains 8 ordered Buzz question slots.
7. Use doppler_spectral_shift-busybee-rubric-metadata.json as the authoritative BusyBee rubric source if custom Meta- fields are not retained by Buzz.

PUBLISHING AND PREVIEW CHECKS

- Alignment records a row, reveals the motion diagram, and preserves delta-lambda without displaying z.
- All three objects can be completed and the optional evidence image unlocks at 3 of 3.
- Questions 1-8 appear in their matching labeled slots and the final two essay questions remain last.
- Keyboard navigation, copy/paste, text selection, and the context menu remain available.
- Teacher-test data is reset before students open the assessment.
- Verify the drag interactions on a touch device as well as with a mouse.
