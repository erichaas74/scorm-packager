UNIT 5 — BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Spectral Shift Investigation

Read these directions before selecting Begin. This activity is your opportunity to practice the lesson concepts and collect the evidence you will need for the assessment.

YOUR GOAL
Match absorption-line fingerprints and measure how far each object's lines have shifted from their rest wavelengths.

BE PREPARED
Have your lesson notes available. You will need a calculator to turn a measured shift into a speed.

WHAT TO DO
1. For each object, drag a reference spectrum into the top slot and compare the line spacing. Testing one that does not fit is a fair way to rule it out.
2. Drag the dashed rest-wavelength markers until they sit on the reference lines above.
3. When the lines align, the measurement records itself. Note the shift and the rest wavelength shown in the readout.
4. Repeat for all three objects.
5. You may select "Download evidence image" to save an optional record.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Keep the measured-shift table visible while answering every assessment question below the lab. Review unanswered items before submitting.

When you understand the directions, select Begin.
