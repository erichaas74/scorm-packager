7) In Wave Interference, turn on two water-wave sources with the same frequency. Describe where constructive and destructive interference occur.
a. A strong answer explains that constructive interference occurs where matching parts of the waves overlap and create larger amplitude, while destructive interference occurs where a crest overlaps a trough and reduces or cancels the displacement.

8) Does destructive interference mean the waves permanently lose their energy? Use an observation from the simulation.
