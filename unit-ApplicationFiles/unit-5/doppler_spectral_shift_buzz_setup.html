Buzz Setup: Spectral Shift Investigation

What this activity is

A self-contained absorption-spectrum investigation with three fixed cases. For each object the student drags a reference element's spectrum into the comparison tool, then drags the dashed rest-wavelength markers until they align with the observed lines. The gap the student opens is the shift, and the readout reports it.

The lab's two built-in multiple-choice steps were removed in the conversion. Aligning the lines now completes the case directly and records the measurement into a table. Buzz owns the classification and the interpretation.

1. Import the 8 questions

  1. Create a Buzz Assessment titled Unit 5 Lesson 5: Spectral Shift Investigation.
  2. Paste all of doppler_spectral_shift_buzz_questions.txt into Buzz's Text editor / Edit all questions screen.
  3. Confirm the import creates 8 questions worth 14 points: one upload, four numeric, one multiple choice, and two essays.

2. Upload the assessment template

  1. Upload doppler-spectral-line-shift/index.html as the assessment template.
  2. The template contains exactly 8 ordered <a:question /> markers.
  3. Turn off question randomization/shuffling and do not use one-question-per-page.

If Buzz strips the embedded script, host the complete HTML and use a teacher-approved external link. The drag interactions require pointer events; verify them on a touch device as well.

3. What the readout shows

The readout reports Δλ and the rest wavelength λ0 only. The line z = Δλ/λ0 was removed in the conversion, because Question 6 asks the student to compute exactly that. λ0 stays visible as given data — the student needs it to do the division.

5. BusyBee essay grading

All 2 essays in this assessment (Questions 7 and 8) are graded by BusyBee, not by hand. Each carries Meta-unit, Meta-lesson, Meta-skill, Meta-grading: busybee, and Meta-evidence tags, an @[Always] line telling the student what is being graded, and a Full/Partial/No credit rubric in the answer line.

The authoritative rubric copy is doppler_spectral_shift-busybee-rubric-metadata.json in this folder. It carries the same rubric criteria plus per-question common misconceptions, revision-oriented feedback strings, and the human-review triggers. If Buzz ignores custom Meta- metadata in your domain, that JSON is the copy of record — load it into BusyBee directly.

Route to a human grader when the response conflicts with the uploaded evidence image, when the student cites values outside the expected range that the image may nonetheless support, or when the reasoning is unusual but could be correct. The full trigger list is in the JSON.

6. Student preview checklist

  1. The comparison SVG renders and the reference cards are draggable (script not stripped).

  3. Landing on the alignment shows the LINES ALIGNED badge, records the row into the Measured shifts table, and reveals the motion diagram.
  4. Complete all three objects; the Download evidence image button unlocks at 3 of 3.
  5. Confirm the readout shows Δλ and λ0 but not z.
  6. Confirm Questions 1-8 appear in the matching labeled slots. If they do not, randomization is still on.
  7. Enter the key values above and confirm all four numeric questions grade correct.
  8. Confirm both essay boxes render and the upload question accepts PNG.
  9. Confirm the visible page title reads Spectral Shift Investigation.
  10. Confirm copy and paste are blocked inside the Buzz question controls, not only in the lab body.
  11. Use Reset after testing so students begin with an empty measurement table.
