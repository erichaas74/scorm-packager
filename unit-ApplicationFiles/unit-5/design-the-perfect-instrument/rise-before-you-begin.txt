UNIT 5 — BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Design the Perfect Instrument

Read these directions before selecting Begin. This activity is your opportunity to practice the lesson concepts and collect the evidence you will need for the assessment.

YOUR GOAL
Build a string and an air-column instrument that produce required target notes, and collect the length, tension, and harmonic that made each note possible.

BE PREPARED
Have your lesson notes available. Use a calculator and scratch paper when the investigation includes calculations or data comparisons.

WHAT TO DO
1. Work through all seven challenges in order.
2. For each one, adjust the controls that are live until the tuner reaches the target note. The build records itself when you reach it.
3. One challenge cannot be tuned to its target. When you are convinced no mode fits, select "No mode fits" to record that result.
4. Watch what changes when the air temperature changes.
5. When all seven builds are recorded, you may select "Download evidence image" to save an optional record.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Keep the recorded-build table visible while answering every assessment question below the lab. Review unanswered items before submitting.

When you understand the directions, select Begin.
