UNIT 5 HONORS — BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Honors Relativity Timekeeping Lab

Read these directions before selecting Begin. This activity is your opportunity to practice the lesson concepts and collect the evidence you will need for the assessment.

YOUR GOAL
Determine how a clock's altitude and speed each change its tick rate, and find the orbit where the two effects cancel exactly.

BE PREPARED
Have your lesson notes available. You will need a calculator for the Lorentz factor and the timing-to-distance conversion. Keep scratch paper for the data table and graph readings.

WHAT TO DO
1. Choose a preset or set the altitude and speed sliders yourself. Use "Snap to circular orbit" so the speed matches what gravity actually requires at that altitude.
2. Select "Record this trial" for each setup you want in your data table.
3. Record at least four trials, including at least one clock that ends up running fast and one that ends up running slow. The evidence image stays locked until you do.
4. Read the zero crossing off the graph, then review the evidence cards for the muon, Hafele-Keating, and Pound-Rebka questions.
5. You may select "Download evidence image" to save an optional record.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Keep the trial table, graph, and evidence cards visible while answering every assessment question below the lab. Review unanswered items before submitting.

When you understand the directions, select Begin.
