UNIT 5 HONORS — BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Honors Relativity Timekeeping Lab

Read these directions before selecting Begin. This activity is your opportunity to practice the lesson concepts and collect the evidence you will need for the assessment.

YOUR GOAL
Determine how a clock's altitude and speed each change its tick rate, and find the orbit where the two effects cancel exactly.

BE PREPARED
Have your lesson notes available. You will need a calculator for the Lorentz factor and the timing-to-distance conversion. Keep scratch paper for the data table and graph readings.

WHAT TO DO
1. Work through the five guide steps, then explore the presets.
2. Record at least four trials, including at least one clock that ends up running fast and one that ends up running slow. The analysis questions stay locked until you do.
3. Use "Snap to circular orbit" so the speed matches what gravity actually requires at that altitude.
4. Read the zero crossing off the graph, then use the evidence cards for the muon, Hafele-Keating, and Pound-Rebka questions.
5. Answer all eight analysis questions. You get two attempts each.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Complete every required investigation step and all required assessment questions. Review unanswered items before submitting. If the lab shows a Submit / Resubmit button, select it and wait for confirmation before returning to Rise.

When you understand the directions, select Begin.
