Grading design:
- Evidence based, with two stable numerics. The simulation's transmission model is fixed:
  white light through any filter transmits exactly 28%, and monochromatic light through a
  matched filter transmits 100%. Those two readings are safe auto-graded numerics (Q3, Q4).
- Q2 matching grades the three additive pairs, which are fixed for every student.
- Q5 and Q6 are fixed conceptual multiple-choice items.
- The Part 7 color-matching slider values vary by student, so they are graded through the
  evidence upload and the Q8 essay, never by a fixed numeric key.
- Q1 is the evidence upload; Q7-Q9 use BusyBee-graded rubrics (style B) and require the
  student to quote their own recorded rows.
