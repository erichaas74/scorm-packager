UNIT 5 — BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Light Color and Vision Lab

Read these directions before selecting Begin. This activity is your opportunity to practice the lesson concepts and collect the evidence you will need for the assessment.

YOUR GOAL
Investigate wavelengths, filters, and color mixing, and record the evidence that shows how the light reaching the eye determines perceived color.

BE PREPARED
Have your lesson notes available. Use scratch paper to note the readings you will need later.

WHAT TO DO
1. Single Bulb workspace: with white light selected, insert and record the red, green, blue, and yellow filters. Watch the transmission readout.
2. RGB Bulbs workspace: turn on each of the seven on/off combinations and record every result.
3. Build and record yellow, cyan, and magenta.
4. Choose each color-matching target, adjust the intensity sliders for the closest match, then record it.
5. When all 18 trials are recorded on the Evidence tab, you may select "Download evidence image" to save an optional record.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Keep the Evidence tab available while answering every assessment question below the lab. Review unanswered items before submitting.

When you understand the directions, select Begin.
