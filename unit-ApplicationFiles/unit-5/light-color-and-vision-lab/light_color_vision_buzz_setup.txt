LIGHT, COLOR, AND VISION LAB
UNIT 5 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Build color the way light and your eyes do! You will filter wavelengths, mix red-green-blue light, and compare cone responses to explain why the same color sensation can come from very different light combinations.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Predict which wavelengths a colored filter transmits or absorbs.
- Explain additive color mixing with red, green, and blue light.
- Connect wavelength mixtures to the relative responses of the eye’s cone cells.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Complete the filter workspace and record every required source-filter trial.
2. Complete the RGB mixing workspace and record the required primary and secondary combinations.
3. Use the color-matching workspace to compare spectra and cone-response patterns until all 18 trials are recorded.
4. Answer the Buzz questions in order and use values from the evidence tables in the final two responses.
5. Download the evidence image only if you want a personal record; it is not submitted or graded.
6. Before submitting, confirm that you answered all 8 questions and that the final two responses cite your recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- Evidence downloads are optional records. No file upload earns or replaces points.
