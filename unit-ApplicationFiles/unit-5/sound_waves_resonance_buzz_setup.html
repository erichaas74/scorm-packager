Buzz Setup: Resonance Tube Lab

What this activity is

A self-contained resonance-tube investigation. Students raise and lower a water-filled pipe under four tuning forks (256, 512, 1024, and 2048 Hz), locate the 1st, 3rd, and 5th harmonic peaks for each, and record 12 air-column lengths. They then convert each length to meters, calculate the wavelength and the wave speed for all 12 trials, average the speeds, and compute a percent difference from 343 m/s.

The lab validates the calculations against each student's own measurements. Every row is checked against that student's recorded length, and the final average is checked against that student's own 12 speeds. Because of this, the row calculations are deliberately not re-graded in Buzz as fixed numeric questions - doing so would grade one student's arithmetic against another student's measurement.

The template contains no formulas. The wavelength and speed relationships were removed from the calculation banner and the table headers, because performing that conversion is the graded skill.

3. Answer key

Resonance peak positions

Peaks sit at L = n × 343 / (4f). The lab accepts a recording only within a narrow band of the true peak, so short air columns are effectively single-valued while the long 256 Hz columns admit a small spread. This is why only the 1st-harmonic lengths of the three higher forks are auto-graded.

4. BusyBee essay grading

All 2 essays in this assessment (Questions 10 and 11) are graded by BusyBee, not by hand. Each carries Meta-unit, Meta-lesson, Meta-skill, Meta-grading: busybee, and Meta-evidence tags, an @[Always] line telling the student what is being graded, and a Full/Partial/No credit rubric in the answer line.

The authoritative rubric copy is sound_waves_resonance-busybee-rubric-metadata.json in this folder. It carries the same rubric criteria plus per-question common misconceptions, revision-oriented feedback strings, and the human-review triggers. If Buzz ignores custom Meta- metadata in your domain, that JSON is the copy of record — load it into BusyBee directly.

Route to a human grader when the response conflicts with the uploaded evidence image, when the student cites values outside the expected range that the image may nonetheless support, or when the reasoning is unusual but could be correct. The full trigger list is in the JSON.
