  dead weight and has been removed. The required values stay readable in the on-screen tables
  and in the evidence PNG; students type the short numbers. Unit 6 Lesson 2 set this precedent.
- Download CSV was kept. It is a file download, not a clipboard action, so the guard does not
  block it and it gives students an offline copy of their own data.
