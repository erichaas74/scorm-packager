THIN LENS INVESTIGATION
UNIT 5 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Make light rays reveal an invisible rule! You will move an object and screen around a converging lens, capture real and virtual images, and test whether the thin-lens equation predicts what the simulation shows.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Distinguish real images from virtual images using ray behavior and screen evidence.
- Apply the thin-lens equation and magnification relationship with correct signs.
- Use multiple trials to explain how object distance changes image position, size, and orientation.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Record three focused real-image trials using the required object distances.
2. Complete the p = 10.0 cm virtual-image trial and explain why no screen measurement exists for it.
3. Calculate and verify the three required application rows before moving to the assessment questions.
4. Answer the Buzz questions in order and cite values from the observation and calculation tables in the final responses.
5. Download the evidence image only if you want a personal record; it is not submitted or graded.
6. Before submitting, confirm that you answered all 8 questions and that the final two responses cite your recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- Evidence downloads are optional records. No file upload earns or replaces points.
