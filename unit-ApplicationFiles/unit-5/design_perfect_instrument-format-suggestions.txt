Completed conversion work (2026-08-10):
- REMOVED all 24 built-in multiple-choice questions and their worked feedback strings.
  The pre, q, f, and post fields were deleted from every entry in the CH array, and the
  showQ, waveCard, answer, and currentQ functions were removed with them. The prediction
  questions stated the boundary conditions and the wavelength fraction BEFORE the student
  built anything, which is exactly what Questions 6 through 9 now grade.
- REMOVED the score counter, TOTALQ, and the postMessage completion signal. The finish
  screen no longer reports "N / 24 correct".
- Reachng a target now calls lockIn -> recordBuild, which stores the challenge title,
  resonator type, target note, length, tension, harmonic, air temperature, and the
  achieved frequency.
- Added a Your recorded builds table and a gated evidence PNG. The PNG unlocks at 7 of 7.
- Added 9 ordered Buzz question slots and the local-file note.
- Added the buzz-assessment-integrity-guard script.
- Softened the "No mode fits" confirmation. It previously read "Correct - no harmonic of
  this pipe reaches the target", which is the answer to Question 6.

IMPORTANT physics change - challenges 4 and 5 locked to the fundamental:
  Both originally used enable:["L","n"], leaving the harmonic slider live. That admits a
  second valid build inside the 1.20 m slider range:
    Challenge 4, open pipe E4:    n=1 at 0.520 m  OR  n=2 at 1.039 m
    Challenge 5, stopped pipe A3: n=1 at 0.390 m  OR  n=3 at 1.169 m
  Either build clears the challenge, so the graded length would have been ambiguous and
  a student who legitimately used the higher mode would be marked wrong. Both are now
  enable:["L"], so every student records the same fundamental length.
  Challenges 2 and 6 still expose the harmonic slider - sweeping the modes is their point.

Deviations from the conversion guide:

  weight. Values stay readable in the builds table and the evidence PNG.

  E4") were KEPT. They are the apparatus and its operating instructions, not solving help;
  no formula or boundary condition is stated anywhere in the template.
