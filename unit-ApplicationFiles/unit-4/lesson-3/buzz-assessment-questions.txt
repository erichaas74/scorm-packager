9) Explain the energy changes in a successful loop trial. Use gravitational potential energy, kinetic energy, and conservation of energy. State why the cart has its greatest speed near the bottom.
a. A strong answer explains that with friction set to zero, total mechanical energy is conserved. As the cart descends, gravitational potential energy becomes kinetic energy, so speed is greatest near the bottom. As the cart rises, kinetic energy becomes gravitational potential energy, but enough kinetic energy must remain at the top to maintain contact.
