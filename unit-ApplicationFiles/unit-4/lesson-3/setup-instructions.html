Buzz Setup: Unit 4 Lesson 3 Loop-the-Loop Energy Investigation

Open the Unit 4 Buzz scoring and publishing guide.

Assessment design

The Buzz template is a fixed-trial assessment. It locks mass to 100 kg, radius to 20 m, gravity to 9.81 m/s2, and friction to zero. Question 1 and the in-app prediction must be completed before trials unlock.

Create the nine questions

  1. Create an assessment titled Unit 4 Lesson 3: Loop-the-Loop Energy Investigation.
  2. Paste all of buzz-assessment-questions.txt into Buzz's text editor.
  3. Confirm nine questions worth 15 points: two multiple-choice, five numeric, and two essay questions.
  4. Confirm the grading split is 10 automatically graded points and 5 BusyBee rubric-graded essay points.

Upload and validate

  1. Upload u4l3-loop-sim-lab-buzz-assessment-template.html.
  2. Keep question order fixed. Question 1 appears before the simulation; Questions 2–9 appear after it.
  3. Use u4l3-loop-sim-lab-buzz-assessment-template-preview.html for local placement review.
  4. Verify that Trial 1 reports no on-track top speed, Trial 2 reports the ideal N = 0 threshold, and Trial 3 reports approximately 981 N.

Turn off question randomization and do not use one-question-per-page.

Publishing fallback

If Buzz strips inline script, host the lab and link or embed it while retaining all nine native Buzz questions. The standalone simulation by itself is practice, not the graded assessment.
