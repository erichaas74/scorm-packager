Buzz Setup: Unit 4 Lesson 3 Loop-the-Loop Energy Investigation

What this activity is

The assessment template is a self-contained, fixed-trial version of the loop simulation. It preserves the track-and-cart observation experience while locking mass, loop radius, gravity, and friction so every student sees the same data. The original Loop-Sim-Lab.html remains the adjustable practice lab.

Step 1: Create the nine questions

  1. Create a Buzz Assessment titled Unit 4 Lesson 3: Loop-the-Loop Energy Investigation.
  2. Paste all of buzz-assessment-questions.txt into Buzz's Text editor / Edit all questions screen.
  3. The file creates nine questions worth 13 points: four multiple choice, three fixed-number questions, and two essays.

Step 2: Upload the template

  1. Upload u4l3-loop-sim-lab-buzz-assessment-template.html.
  2. The template has nine <a:question></a:question> slots. Keep the imported questions in their authored order.
  3. Open the generated local preview before uploading to see native-question placement.

Turn OFF question randomization and do NOT use one-question-per-page. Buzz fills slots in display order; randomizing questions breaks the assessment layout.

Step 3: Preview as a student

  1. Confirm all three trial tabs run an animated cart and fill their observation row.
  2. Confirm Trial 1 ends with a fall and Trials 2 and 3 complete.
  3. Confirm Questions 1 through 9 appear in sequence under the lab.
  4. Enter 29.71 m/s, 14.01 m/s, and 58,860 J for Questions 2, 4, and 6 to confirm numeric grading.
  5. If the animation does not draw, Buzz stripped inline script. Use the complete Loop-Sim-Lab.html as the hosted fallback.
