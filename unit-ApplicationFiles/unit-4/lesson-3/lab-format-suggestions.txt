Lab Format Notes: Unit 4 Lesson 3 - Loop-the-Loop Energy Investigation

Current lab format
- The original Loop-Sim-Lab.html remains the adjustable standalone practice simulation.
- The Buzz template locks cart mass to 100 kg, loop radius to 20 m, gravity to 9.81 m/s2, and friction to 0.00.
- Fixed release-height trials make all Buzz numeric and observation questions reliably gradeable.

Fixed reference values
- Trial 1: 45 m release; bottom speed 29.71 m/s; top speed 9.90 m/s; cart loses contact and falls.
- Trial 2: 50 m release; bottom speed 31.32 m/s; top speed 14.01 m/s; minimum successful loop trial.
- Trial 3: 60 m release; bottom speed 34.31 m/s; top speed 19.81 m/s; completes with excess speed.
- Trial 3 initial potential energy: mgh = (100 kg)(9.81 m/s2)(60 m) = 58,860 J.

Publishing notes

- The template shows observations but does not give formulas or worked explanations.
