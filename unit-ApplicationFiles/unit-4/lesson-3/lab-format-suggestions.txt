Lab Format Notes: Unit 4 Lesson 3 - Loop-the-Loop Energy Investigation

Current lab format
- Loop-Sim-Lab.html remains the adjustable standalone practice simulation.
- The Buzz template uses three fixed frictionless trials and requires a prediction before trials unlock.
- Progress persists for the current browser tab.

Fixed reference values
- Trial 1: 45 m release; bottom speed 29.71 m/s; detaches at 36.67 m with speed 12.79 m/s. It has no on-track top speed.
- Trial 2: 50 m release; bottom speed 31.32 m/s; top speed 14.01 m/s; ideal h = 2.5R threshold with N = 0 at the top.
- Trial 3: 60 m release; bottom speed 34.31 m/s; top speed 19.81 m/s; top normal force approximately 981 N.
- Trial 3 initial potential energy is 58,860 J.

Grading notes
- Nine questions total 15 points.
- Ten points are automatically graded; five use BusyBee-supported rubrics.
- Trial 1 must never be assigned a measured top speed.
- Energy conservation and the radial-force contact condition are assessed separately.

Publishing notes
- The template does not block copy, paste, context menus, or accessibility tools.
- Keep question order fixed because Question 1 is placed before the simulation.
