Unit 4 Buzz Assessment Instructions

Required setup and scoring standard for the completed Unit 4 applications.

Required scoring

Every completed Unit 4 application assessment is worth 15 points: 10 automatically graded points and 5 BusyBee rubric-graded essay points.

Create each Buzz assessment

  1. Open the lesson's buzz-assessment-questions.txt file and paste the complete contents into the Buzz assessment question editor.
  2. Keep the authored question order. Turn off question randomization and do not use one-question-per-page.
  3. Confirm the question count and verify that the Score: values total exactly 15 points.
  4. Upload the lesson's *-buzz-assessment-template.html file as the assessment template.
  5. Use the matching *-preview.html file to confirm every native question appears in the intended slot.
  6. Set the Buzz activity's maximum score to 15 points if Buzz does not calculate it automatically.

BusyBee grading

  • The final two essay questions in each assessment total 5 points: a 2-point evidence response and a 3-point conclusion/CER response.
  • Keep the Meta-grading: busybee, evidence metadata, and authored grading-feedback lines with each essay.
  • Grade only the evidence requested in that lesson. Do not award full credit for unsupported claims or evidence copied from a different trial.

Student-preview checklist

  • The application loads and the fixed assessment data match the lesson-specific setup instructions.
  • Required predictions, trials, calculations, and progress gates work before students reach the related questions.
  • All questions are visible, in order, and total 15 points.
  • Numeric questions accept the authored tolerance ranges.
  • Copy, paste, keyboard navigation, and assistive technology are not blocked.
  • The application records completion only; native Buzz questions determine the assessment score.
