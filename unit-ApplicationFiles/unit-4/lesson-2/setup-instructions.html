Buzz Setup: Unit 4 Lesson 2 Hooke's Law Lab

Open the Unit 4 Buzz scoring and publishing guide.

Package contents

  • hookes-law-lab.html: adjustable standalone practice lab.
  • u4l2-hookes-law-buzz-assessment-template.html: self-contained, fixed Spring A assessment.
  • u4l2-hookes-law-buzz-assessment-template-preview.html: local question-placement preview.
  • buzz-assessment-questions.txt: ten questions worth 15 points.

Step 1: Create the assessment

  1. Create an assessment titled Unit 4 Lesson 2: Hooke's Law Lab.
  2. Paste all of buzz-assessment-questions.txt into Buzz's text editor.
  3. Confirm ten questions worth 15 points: four multiple-choice, four numeric, and two essay questions.
  4. Confirm the grading split is 10 automatically graded points and 5 BusyBee rubric-graded essay points.
  5. Keep the authored order so each native question fills its matching template slot.

Turn off question randomization. Do not use one question per page.

Step 2: Upload and preview

  1. Upload u4l2-hookes-law-buzz-assessment-template.html as the assessment template.
  2. Confirm Spring A is the only selectable spring, the mystery mass is fixed, and Reveal actual k is unavailable.
  3. Confirm the best-fit button remains disabled until five unique known masses are recorded.
  4. Confirm all ten native questions appear below the lab.

Student workflow

  1. Predict the force-extension relationship.
  2. Record all five known masses. Extension is measured to 0.001 m.
  3. Calculate and enter k before selecting Calculate best-fit line.
  4. Attach the mystery mass, calculate its mass using m = kx/g, and check it in the lab.
  5. Use the table, slope, R2, and mystery result in the final responses.

Validation

  • Use 1.962 N for Question 2, approximately 20.0 N/m for Question 4, 0.250 kg for Question 6, and 0.100 J for Question 8.
  • The application records progress; native Buzz questions determine the score.
  • If inline script is stripped, host hookes-law-lab.html and keep the native Buzz questions in the assessment.
