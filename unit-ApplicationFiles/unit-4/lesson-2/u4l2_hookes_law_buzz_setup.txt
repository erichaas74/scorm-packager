HOOKE'S LAW LAB
UNIT 4 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Measure spring stretch and use the force-extension relationship to determine an unknown mass.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Organize five force-extension measurements with consistent units.
- Calculate a spring constant and compare it with a best-fit slope.
- Use Hooke's law and evidence to infer a mystery mass.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Predict the force-extension relationship.
2. Record all five unique known masses for Spring A, measuring extension to 0.001 m.
3. Calculate and enter a finite positive spring-constant estimate before selecting Calculate best-fit line.
4. Attach the mystery mass and calculate its mass using m = kx/g.
5. Answer the questions using your measurements, slope, fit quality, and mystery-mass result.
6. Before submitting through Buzz, confirm that all 10 questions are answered and the final two responses cite your evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- No file upload earns or replaces points.

- BusyBee scoring: Award points only in 0.5-point increments, including whole points, from 0 to the question maximum. Select a rubric-supported allowed score; never report hundredths such as 2.85/3. Do not average or percentage-scale rubric criteria into an off-grid score. A binary completion rubric still permits only 0 or full credit.
