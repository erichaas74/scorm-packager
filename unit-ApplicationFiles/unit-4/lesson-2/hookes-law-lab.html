Use hanging masses and Hooke’s law to determine the spring constant from the slope of a force–extension graph.

Hooke’s law: Fs = −kx. At equilibrium, the magnitudes satisfy mg = kx, so the slope of a graph of F versus x is the spring constant k.

Suggested procedure

  1. Remove the mass and identify the spring’s unstretched length.
  2. Add at least five different masses without exceeding the red limit line.
  3. Wait for the mass to settle, then select Record Trial.
  4. Create a best-fit line on the force-versus-extension graph.
  5. Use the slope to determine the experimental spring constant.
The red line marks the spring’s recommended maximum extension. Trials beyond that line are not recorded.
