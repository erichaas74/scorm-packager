Lab procedure and physics
  1. Note the unstretched length, L0 = 0.180 m.
  2. Choose one unknown spring. Changing springs clears the current data set.
  3. Drag each known mass to the apparatus. Its extension records automatically to the nearest 0.001 m at equilibrium.
  4. Create a best-fit line on the force-versus-extension graph.
  5. Use the slope as the experimental spring constant, k, then use the mystery mass's extension to investigate its mass.
