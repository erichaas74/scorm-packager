Lab Format Notes: Unit 4 Lesson 2 - Which Track Is Fastest?

Current lab format
- Buzz-native prediction and investigation with four simultaneous carts on different paths.
- Live timer for each cart, speed labels at 25/50/75 percent markers, stacked energy bars, and velocity plus centripetal-acceleration vectors on curved tracks.
- Controls include gravity, mass, friction on/off, wheel rotational inertia, starting height, and dip depth.
- Replay preserves the selected setup. Slow motion changes playback rate but not recorded physics time.
- Results include finish time, final speed, average speed, path length, and three marker speeds, with copy and CSV options.
- Eight Buzz questions cover prediction, fair test, data, equal-height energy, travel time, vectors, variable tests, and conclusion.

Physics model notes
- Each track is discretized into 800 path segments. Arc length, friction work, speed, curvature, and elapsed time are calculated segment by segment.
- Total kinetic energy is 0.5mv^2(1 + beta), where beta = I/(mr^2). Translational and rotational energy bars split this total.
- With friction off, v_final depends only on gravity, start-to-finish height change, and beta, so track shape and mass do not change final speed.
- Travel time depends on the complete speed history. A track that creates speed earlier can have a higher average speed.
- Rolling resistance is proportional to path length. Longer paths therefore lose more energy when friction is on.
- A deeper dip can reduce time at first, but enough added depth lengthens the path and can reverse the advantage.

Teacher and BusyBee grading notes
- Student results legitimately vary with settings. Grade tables for completeness, common setup, units, and internally consistent relationships.
- Mass changes energy values in joules but should not change ideal speed or time.
- Increased rotational inertia should reduce translational speed because more energy enters wheel rotation.
- Centripetal acceleration changes velocity direction on curves; it does not add mechanical energy.
- Suggested total is 25 points.
