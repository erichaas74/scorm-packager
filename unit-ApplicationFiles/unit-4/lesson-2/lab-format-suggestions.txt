Lab Format Notes: Unit 4 Lesson 2 - Hooke's Law Lab

Current lab format
- The standalone practice lab allows Springs A, B, and C and a randomly selected mystery mass.
- The Buzz assessment locks Spring A at k = 20 N/m and the mystery mass at 0.250 kg so numeric keys are stable.
- Five known masses are required: 0.050, 0.100, 0.200, 0.300, and 0.500 kg.
- Extension records to the nearest 0.001 m, making graph interpretation and measurement resolution meaningful.
- Learners calculate k before the application displays the regression slope.

Grading notes
- Ten questions total 15 points.
- Ten points are automatically graded; five use BusyBee-supported rubrics.
- Numeric tolerances accommodate the displayed measurement resolution.
- The final claim is limited to the tested elastic range.

Publishing notes
- Do not randomize question order or use one-question-per-page.
- The application completion state is not a score. Native Buzz questions determine the grade.
- Verify all five trials, the fit, and the fixed mystery mass in Buzz student preview.
