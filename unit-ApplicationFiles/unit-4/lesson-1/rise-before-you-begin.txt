UNIT 4 - LESSON 1 - BEFORE YOU BEGIN
IMA and AMA Lab

YOUR GOAL
Acquire force and distance data, calculate ideal mechanical advantage (IMA) and actual mechanical advantage (AMA), and compare the two values.

BE PREPARED
Have a calculator and scratch paper available. Round calculated mechanical advantage to the nearest hundredth.

WHAT TO DO
1. Review the three configurations for the selected simple machine.
2. Run all three trials to acquire input/output force and distance measurements.
3. Calculate IMA = input distance / output distance.
4. Calculate AMA = output force / input force.
5. Enter and check both calculations for Setups A, B, and C.
6. Repeat for all four simple machines, then answer the Buzz questions.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Confirm that all four machine levels are marked complete and answer every assessment question before submitting.
