SIX SIMPLE MACHINES WORK LAB
UNIT 4 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Use six adjustable machines to explain how mechanical advantage connects force, distance, work, and speed.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Calculate ideal mechanical advantage from physical settings.
- Compare input force and distance while keeping ideal work constant.
- Use calculations from several machines to explain output speed.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Use the shared givens: output force 120 N, output distance 1.0 m, and input speed 1.0 m/s.
2. Work through inclined plane, lever, wedge, wheel and axle, pulley, and screw in order.
3. Compare the two settings named in each pair of questions. Calculate IMA, input force, input distance, ideal work, and output speed using the provided relationships.
4. Answer the two questions beneath each machine. Use the coarse and fine screw calculations and at least two other machines in your final responses.
5. Before submitting through Buzz, confirm that all 12 questions are answered and the final two responses cite your evidence.
