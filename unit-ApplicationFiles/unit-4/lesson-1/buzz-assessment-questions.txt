6) Why can three setups of the same simple machine have equal output work but different completion times and powers?
a. Equal work means all three setups must also have equal time and power.
*b. They lift the same mass through the same height, but their geometry changes input force and distance; at the shared input speed this changes time and the rate of doing work.
c. The slower setup destroys part of the load's gravitational potential energy.
d. The setup with the greatest mechanical advantage creates extra energy.
