Question 9

Buzz Question 9 E - 3 points
Type: EScore: 3Height: 240
9) Explain why the single-wall metal bottle transferred thermal energy faster than the vacuum-insulated bottle. Use conduction, insulation, and thermal energy in your explanation.
A

A strong answer explains that metal is a good conductor, so thermal energy moves through a single metal wall more easily. A vacuum-insulated bottle reduces conduction through its walls because the insulating vacuum greatly limits particle-to-particle energy transfer.

Question 10

Buzz Question 10 E - 3 points
Type: EScore: 3Height: 260
10) Explain how the lid changes thermal transfer in the vacuum-insulated bottle. Use convection, radiation, and thermal equilibrium in your answer.
A

A strong answer explains that an open bottle allows warm air and water vapor to circulate out and cooler air to circulate in, increasing convection. A lid also reduces heat loss from the opening, including radiation. Neither bottle stops transfer completely, so the water eventually approaches thermal equilibrium with the room.
