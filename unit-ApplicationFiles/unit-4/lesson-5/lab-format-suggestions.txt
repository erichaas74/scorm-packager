Lab Format Notes: Unit 4 Lesson 5 - Water Bottle Thermal Test

Current lab format
- Standalone practice and Buzz assessment use the same four fixed simulated trials.
- Controlled values are 0.50 kg water, 80.0 C starting temperature, 22.0 C room temperature, and 60 minutes.
- A prediction is required before trials unlock, and completion persists in the browser tab.
- The accessible tab buttons, run button, text table, and status messages provide non-canvas access to every result.

Fixed reference values
- Plastic with lid: 80, 68, 59, 52, 48 C.
- Single-wall metal with lid: 80, 62, 51, 43, 39 C.
- Vacuum-insulated with lid: 80, 77, 75, 73, 72 C.
- Vacuum-insulated with lid removed: 80, 73, 69, 66, 64 C.
- Vacuum-with-lid water energy change: -16.744 kJ.
- Metal average temperature-change rate: -0.683 C/min.

Grading notes
- Ten questions total 15 points.
- Ten points are automatically graded; five use BusyBee-supported rubrics.
- The wall comparison supports a conduction explanation. The lid comparison supports convection and evaporation. The data do not separately measure radiation.

Publishing notes
- The assessment template does not block clipboard commands, context menus, or accessibility tools.
- Keep question order fixed because Question 1 appears before the simulation.
