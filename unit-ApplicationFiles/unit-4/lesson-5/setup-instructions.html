Buzz Setup: Unit 4 Lesson 5 Water Bottle Thermal Test

What this activity is

This self-contained template has four fixed, animated bottle trials. Learners run each 60-minute time-lapse and collect the temperature table. All trials use 0.50 kg of water at 80.0 C in a 22.0 C room, so the numeric answers are identical for every student. The template deliberately contains no formula or solution guidance; the energy-transfer calculation and thermal-transfer explanations belong to the graded questions.

Step 1: Create the ten questions

  1. Create a Buzz Assessment titled Unit 4 Lesson 5: Water Bottle Thermal Test.
  2. Paste the entire contents of buzz-assessment-questions.txt into Buzz's Text editor / Edit all questions screen.
  3. The import creates 10 questions worth 15 points: three multiple choice, four numeric, and three essays. The essay a. lines are teacher grading guides.

Question 5 key: Q = (0.50 kg)(4.186 kJ/(kg C))(72.0 C - 80.0 C) = -16.744 kJ. The accepted range is -17.0 to -16.5 kJ.

Step 2: Upload the template

  1. Upload u4l5-water-bottle-thermal-test-buzz-assessment-template.html as the assessment template.
  2. It contains ten <a:question></a:question> slots. The questions file order must match the slot order.
  3. Use u4l5-water-bottle-thermal-test-buzz-assessment-template-preview.html to inspect placement locally.

Turn OFF randomize/shuffle question order. Buzz fills slots in display order, so randomization breaks the question-to-answer alignment. Do not use one-question-per-page.

Step 3: Preview as a student

  1. Confirm all four trial tabs work, each time-lapse draws its bottle and graph, and each completed trial fills the table.
  2. Confirm Question 1 through Question 10 appear in order beneath the lab. A script-free or blank preview means Buzz stripped the template script; use a hosted version of the full template as the fallback.
  3. Enter 48 C, 39 C, 72 C, and -16.744 kJ for Questions 2 through 5 to confirm numeric grading.
  4. Confirm the final three essay boxes are visible and allow responses.
  5. Confirm the page title identifies Water Bottle Thermal Test, catching accidental upload of another lesson's template.
