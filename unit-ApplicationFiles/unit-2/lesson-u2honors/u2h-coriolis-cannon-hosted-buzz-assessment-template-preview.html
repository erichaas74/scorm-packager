A

A strong answer compares the two radial launch directions with trial evidence and relates the different apparent paths to changing distance from the rotation axis and the rotating reference frame.

Buzz Question 5 E - 2 points
Type: EScore: 2Height: 240
5) Explain why the shell follows a straight horizontal path in an inertial overhead frame while an observer rotating with Earth sees a curved path. Use inertia, reference frame, and Coriolis effect in your explanation.
A

A strong answer states that no sideways force is required to keep the shell moving inertially, while the ground, cannon, target, and observer rotate beneath it; the changing rotating coordinate system makes the shell appear to curve, which is described as the Coriolis effect.
