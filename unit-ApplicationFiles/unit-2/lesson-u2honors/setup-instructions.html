Buzz Setup: Unit 2 Honors Coriolis Cannon Investigation

1. What this activity is

The template contains the rotating polar top view, launch-to-target side view, four launch directions, student aim correction, a six-column trial log, copy support, and evidence-image download. Nine Buzz question slots appear below the lab.

The CDN-dependent 3D globe is deliberately absent from the self-contained Buzz upload. The standalone coriolis-effect.html remains the full-globe practice/fallback version.

2. Hosting

Nothing must be hosted for the normal Buzz version. Upload the template directly. If Buzz strips template scripts, host coriolis-effect.html outside Buzz and replace the embedded lab with a hosted link or iframe.

Optional full-globe version: host coriolis-effect.html and scorm-wrapper.js together at a public HTTPS address. Open u2h-coriolis-cannon-hosted-buzz-assessment-template.html in a text editor and replace both instances of REPLACE_WITH_HOSTED_SIMULATION_URL with the public URL of coriolis-effect.html. Then upload that hosted template instead of the self-contained template.

3. Create the questions

  1. Create an Assessment titled Unit 2 Honors: Coriolis Cannon Investigation.
  2. Paste all of buzz-assessment-questions.txt into the Buzz text editor.
  3. Confirm nine questions and 14 total points: four multiple-choice questions at 1 point each, four essays at 2 points each, and one 2-point upload.
  4. Use manual grading for the evidence-based essays; student aim values depend on the selected conditions and trials.

4. Upload the template

Upload u2h-coriolis-cannon-buzz-assessment-template.html.

For the optional hosted full-globe version, upload u2h-coriolis-cannon-hosted-buzz-assessment-template.html after replacing both placeholder URLs.

Turn OFF question randomization/shuffling. The six slots fill in display order. Also turn off one-question-per-page so the lab and all response slots remain together.

5. Preview as a student

  • Confirm the visible title is “Coriolis Cannon Challenge” and the browser/template title identifies Unit 2 Honors.
  • Confirm both canvases animate and all four launch-direction buttons work.
  • Confirm completed shots appear in the trial log.
  • Confirm Copy values and Download Evidence Image work.
