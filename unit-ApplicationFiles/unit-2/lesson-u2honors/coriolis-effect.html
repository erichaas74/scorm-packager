Mini lesson: Why does the path curve?

Read these three ideas before testing the four launch directions.

Inertia + rotating Earth
1Inertia keeps it moving

After the shell leaves the cannon, it keeps its forward motion. It does not automatically follow the rotating ground and target beneath it.

2Rotation changes the observed path

The shell and Earth move in different ways. From the rotating surface, the shell appears to curve right in this Northern Hemisphere model.

3Air resistance opposes motion

In the real atmosphere, drag pushes against the shell's velocity. It slows the shell and reduces its height and range, especially at higher speeds.

Model boundary: This lab isolates inertia and Earth's rotation using a simplified flight arc. Air resistance is an important real-world force, but it is not calculated in this animation.
