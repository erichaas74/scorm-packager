CORIOLIS CANNON CHALLENGE
UNIT 2 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Aim a cannon from a rotating world and outsmart an apparent sideways deflection! You will compare four launch directions, correct your aim, and reconcile what inertial and rotating observers see.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Predict Northern Hemisphere Coriolis deflection for four launch directions.
- Use trial-and-correction evidence to hit a target in a rotating frame.
- Explain why inertial and rotating observers describe different paths.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Run direct-aim trials in all four launch directions and record each miss direction.
2. Choose at least one direction and adjust aim opposite the observed deflection until you hit.
3. Review the two frame views and the completed trial log.
4. Answer all nine Buzz questions in order.
5. Cite the four-direction pattern and a corrected hit in the final two responses.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 9 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- No file upload earns or replaces points.
