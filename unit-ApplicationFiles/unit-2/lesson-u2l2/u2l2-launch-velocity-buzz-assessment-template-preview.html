Projectile Launch Lab: Finding Launch Velocity

Collect Real Data or Use Simulated Data

Use a real ball launch if you can. If you cannot safely record a throw at home, click Use Simulated Data and use those values in the Buzz questions.

Simulated data is acceptable only when a real launch is not possible.

Student Directions

Objective: Experimentally determine the initial launch velocity of a ball by collecting time and distance data.

Materials

  • A ball such as a tennis ball, football, baseball, or lacrosse ball.
  • Measuring tape or a football field with yard markers.
  • Stopwatch or video recording with timestamp.

Procedure

  1. Choose a safe launch site with clear measurement markings.
  2. Aim for a launch angle between 30 and 45 degrees.
  3. Launch the ball once. Repeat only if the measurement does not seem accurate.
  4. Record the total flight time from launch to landing.
  5. Measure the horizontal distance from the launch point to the landing point. Record distance in meters.
  6. If you cannot safely complete the real launch, use the simulated data button above and say that in Question 1.
