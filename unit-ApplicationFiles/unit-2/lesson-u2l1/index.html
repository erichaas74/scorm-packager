Unit 2 Lesson 1: PhET Projectile Launch Lab

Open PhET Projectile Motion

Use the PhET simulation to complete the guided Part A and Part B trials, then return to Buzz for the graded questions.

Buzz Lab Directions

Use the Intro screen and turn air resistance off. In Part A, set the launch height to 12 m and angle to 0 degrees, then test speeds of 0, 5, 10, and 20 m/s. In Part B, reset to ground level and test 90 degrees / 13 m/s, 60 degrees / 15 m/s, and 30 degrees / 26 m/s. Use the measuring tool to record time, range, and maximum height, then answer the Buzz assessment questions.
