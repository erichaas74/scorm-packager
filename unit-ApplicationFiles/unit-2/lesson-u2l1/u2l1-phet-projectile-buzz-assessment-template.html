Student Directions

Objective: Use PhET Projectile Motion to investigate how horizontal velocity, launch height, and vertical velocity affect projectile motion.

Part A: Horizontal Motion From the Same Height

  1. Open the Intro screen and turn air resistance off.
  2. Set launch height to 12 m and angle to 0 degrees.
  3. Run trials at 0 m/s, 5 m/s, 10 m/s, and 20 m/s.
  4. Use the measuring tool to record time of flight and horizontal range where requested.

Part B: Matching Maximum Heights

  1. Reset the cannon to ground level.
  2. Run these trials: 90 degrees at 13 m/s, 60 degrees at 15 m/s, and 30 degrees at 26 m/s.
  3. Use the measuring tool to record the maximum height for each trial.
  4. Use your data to explain what controls horizontal range and maximum height.
