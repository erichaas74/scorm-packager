Buzz Question 9 E - 2 points
Type: EScore: 2Height: 220
9) In 2 to 3 sentences, explain why 45 degrees is the ideal launch angle for greatest range in the Level 2 simulator.
A

A strong answer should explain that the simulator assumes no air resistance and equal launch and landing heights. At 45 degrees, the horizontal and vertical parts of velocity are balanced in a way that maximizes horizontal range.

Buzz Question 10 E - 2 points
Type: EScore: 2Height: 260
10) In baseball, the ideal angle for maximum hit distance is usually much less than 45 degrees. Explain why real baseball does not match the simple Level 2 simulator.
A

A strong answer should explain that baseball has air resistance, spin, ball speed limits, bat-ball collision effects, and launch-height differences. Because the ball loses speed to drag and can get lift or drop from spin, the best real launch angle is usually lower than the no-air-resistance 45 degree result.
