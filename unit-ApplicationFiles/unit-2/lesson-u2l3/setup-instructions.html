Step 2: Create the Buzz assessment questions

  1. Create a Buzz Assessment titled Unit 2 Lesson 3: Circus Launch Range Lab.
  2. Open buzz-assessment-questions.txt and paste the full contents into the Buzz Assessment text editor or Edit all questions screen.
  3. The file contains ten scored questions: four Level 1 complementary-angle questions and six Level 2 range/reflection questions.
  4. The first three questions use Buzz variables and grade the answer as 90 - launch angle.
  5. Questions 5-7 use fixed Level 2 angles of 30 degrees, 45 degrees, and 60 degrees with numeric range tolerances. The simulator's Level 2 angle slider uses 5-degree steps.
  6. Questions 9-10 are written explanations about the ideal simulator range angle and why baseball has a lower ideal launch angle.
  7. Keep the questions in the exact order shown. Each <a:question></a:question> slot pulls in the next Buzz question.

Step 3: Preview the Buzz template

  1. Upload buzz-assessment-template-for-buzz.html as the assessment template in Buzz.
  2. Preview as a student and confirm the simulator, directions, and ten native Buzz questions appear.
