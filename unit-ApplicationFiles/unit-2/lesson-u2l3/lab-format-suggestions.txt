Lab Format Suggestions: Unit 2 Lesson 3 - Circus Launch Complementary Angles

Current fit for the new Buzz format
- Strong fit for a Buzz-owned assessment. Buzz generates the Cannon 1 launch angle, and the simulator lets students discover the Cannon 2 angle.
- The first three Buzz questions are auto-graded numeric answers using the complementary angle rule: answer = 90 - launch angle.
- The fourth Buzz question is a typed response where students explain that matching-range launch angles are complementary.
- Questions 5-10 use Level 2 to test 30, 45, and 60 degree ranges, compare the greatest range, and explain why baseball's real ideal launch angle is lower than the no-air-resistance simulator result.

Current Buzz files
- buzz-assessment-questions.txt: ten native Buzz questions in import-ready format.
- buzz-assessment-template-for-buzz.html: self-contained student layout with the simulator embedded directly, directions, and ten question slots.
- buzz-assessment-template-preview.html: local preview of the Buzz layout.
- setup-instructions.html: teacher setup workflow.
- newer-circus-launches.html: simulator using the newer circus graphics and Buzz-angle workflow.
- newer-circus-launches.html also includes Level 2: a bottom-cannon-only greatest-range exploration where the Cannon 1 slider moves in 5-degree steps, each performer changes outfit before launch, and each launched performer remains standing at the landing range with the launch angle shown above the performer.

Suggested changes before publishing
1. Upload buzz-assessment-template-for-buzz.html as the Buzz assessment template. It no longer needs newer-circus-launches.html as a separate resource.
2. Preview the template in Buzz and confirm the embedded simulator runs. If Buzz strips scripts from templates, host newer-circus-launches.html outside Buzz and switch the template back to a hosted link or iframe.
3. Test the three Buzz variable ranges:
   - Trial 1 launch angle: 20..35 degrees.
   - Trial 2 launch angle: 36..44 degrees.
   - Trial 3 launch angle: 46..70 degrees.
4. Confirm Buzz accepts eval(90 - variable) as the numeric answer key in your domain.
5. No SCORM package is needed. Buzz owns the score.
6. Preview Questions 5-10 in Buzz and confirm the Level 2 slider, 5-degree angle steps, colored launched performers, and range values work correctly.
