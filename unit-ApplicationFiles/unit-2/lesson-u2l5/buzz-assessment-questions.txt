3) Explain why the penny falls into the cup during the quick pull but stays on the card during the slow pull. Use the words inertia and friction in your answer.
a. A strong answer explains that during a quick pull, inertia keeps the penny nearly at rest while the card is pulled away, so gravity drops the penny into the cup. During a slow pull, friction has enough time to pull the penny along with the card.

8) Explain why the slow pull breaks the top string. Use the words weight, pull, and tension in your answer.

9) Explain why the quick pull breaks the bottom string instead of the top string. Use the word inertia in your answer.
a. A strong answer explains that the mass resists the sudden change in motion because of inertia. The quick yank creates a large tension spike in the bottom string before the force fully transfers through the mass to the top string.
