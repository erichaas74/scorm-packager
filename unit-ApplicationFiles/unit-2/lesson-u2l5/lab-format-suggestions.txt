Lab Format Suggestions: Unit 2 Lesson 5 - Inertia and Tension Demo

Current fit for the new Buzz format
- Strong fit for a Buzz-owned assessment because the demo has fixed, observable results.
- The self-contained Buzz template embeds the inertia-tension canvas demo directly above the native Buzz questions.
- Level 1 is the cup, index card, and penny demo. Students compare a slow pull, where the penny stays on the card, with a quick pull, where the penny falls into the cup.
- Level 2 is the string-tension demo. Students run Slow Pull and Fast Pull, then answer questions about which string breaks, the tension values, and why inertia changes the quick-pull result.

Current Buzz files
- buzz-assessment-questions.txt: eleven import-ready Buzz questions.
- buzz-assessment-template-for-buzz.html: self-contained Level 1 and Level 2 demos, a top stage bar for switching demos, directions, and eleven Buzz question slots.
- buzz-assessment-template-preview.html: local preview of the Buzz layout with placeholder question labels.
- setup-instructions.html: teacher setup workflow.
- inertia-tension-demo.html: standalone version used from the Unit 2 menu.

Suggested changes before publishing
1. Upload buzz-assessment-template-for-buzz.html as the Buzz assessment template.
2. Paste buzz-assessment-questions.txt into the Buzz assessment text editor.
3. Preview as a student and confirm the stage bar switches cleanly between Level 1 and Level 2, then confirm Slow Pull and Fast Pull run inside Buzz for both the cup/card/penny demo and the string demo.
4. Confirm Question 5 accepts about 24.8 N and Question 7 accepts about 45.0 N.
5. Require an uploaded screenshot showing one completed pull trial or the completed cup/card/penny result.
6. Keep the old Coriolis lesson under lesson-u2honors as the honors project, not Unit 2 Lesson 5.
