U2 L4: River Rescue

Complete each River Rescue level in order. The matching level is shown above the questions that use it.

Level 1: Straight Shot

Aim upstream so the swimmer's velocity cancels the current and the actual path lands in the ring straight across from the dock.

Questions 1-4

Answer the Level 1 questions after landing in the ring. Open DATA before recording your final telemetry.

Level 2: Fastest Crossing

Reach the far bank anywhere within 11.0 seconds. Choose the heading that maximizes the across-river velocity component.

Questions 5-7

Answer the Level 2 questions after reaching the far bank within the time limit.

Level 3: Aim at the Baby

Use relative motion to aim directly at the baby's current position while the same current carries both of you downstream.

Questions 8-10

Answer the Level 3 questions after a successful rescue. Open DATA to record your final telemetry.

Level 4: Choose the Rescue Speed

Keep the fixed 0 degree heading and find a swim speed that misses the upper boulder but reaches the baby before the lower rock.

Questions 11-13

Answer the Level 4 questions after a successful rescue. Open DATA and capture the success message for your upload.
