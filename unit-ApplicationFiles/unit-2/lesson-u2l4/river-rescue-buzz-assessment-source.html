U2 L4: River Rescue

Complete each River Rescue level in order. The matching level is shown above the questions that use it.

Level 1: Straight Shot

GOAL: land in the ring straight across from the dock. The current drags you downstream, so aim upstream to cancel the drift.

Questions 1-3

Answer the Level 1 questions after landing in the ring. Open DATA before recording your final telemetry.

Level 2: Fastest Crossing

GOAL: reach the far bank ANYWHERE within 11.0 s. Drifting downstream is fine here — speed is everything. Open DATA to watch your time.

Questions 4-6

Answer the Level 2 questions after reaching the far bank within the time limit.

Level 3: Aim at the Baby

TRICK: the baby starts far above you. Choose the angle that points directly at the baby's current position.

Questions 7-8

Answer the Level 3 questions after a successful rescue. Open DATA to record your final telemetry.

Level 4: Choose the Rescue Speed

SPEED CHALLENGE: heading is fixed at 0°. Too slow and the baby hits the lower rock. Too fast and you hit the upper boulder. Only a band of speeds works — find one and record it.

Questions 9-12

Answer the Level 4 questions after a successful rescue. Open DATA to check your telemetry before you record your speed.
