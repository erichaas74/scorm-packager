Buzz Assessment Questions: Unit 2 Lesson 4 - River Rescue

Assessment setup
- Student opens the hosted River Rescue lab from the Buzz directions.
- Student completes Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 in the simulation.
- Student records settings and telemetry, uploads evidence, and answers relative-motion reflection questions.
- Suggested total: 12 points.
- Important teacher note: The current lab allows many successful settings, so most numeric responses should be checked against the student's screenshot unless a fixed Buzz mode is added.

Question 1: Level 1 Settings
Type: Short answer
Points: 1
Prompt: Record the Level 1 settings you used: swim speed, heading angle, river current speed, and river width.
Grading guide: Full credit if all four settings are included and match the uploaded evidence.

Question 2: Level 1 Telemetry
Type: Short answer
Points: 1
Prompt: After clearing Level 1, record your final time, ground speed, downstream drift, and stamina left.
Grading guide: Full credit if the values are complete and match the uploaded evidence.

Question 3: Resultant Velocity Components
Type: Essay or numeric response set
Points: 2
Prompt: Use your Level 1 settings to explain how the swimmer's velocity relative to the water and the river current combined to make the ground velocity.
Grading guide: Full credit includes the across-river component, downstream/upstream component, and the idea that ground velocity is the vector sum of swimmer velocity plus current.

Question 4: Level 2 Strategy
Type: Essay
Points: 2
Prompt: What did you change in Level 2 to avoid the debris and still reach the finish ring? Use your heading or drift data as evidence.
Grading guide: Full credit describes a specific adjustment and connects it to changing the resultant path.

Question 5: Level 3 Outbound and Return Settings
Type: Short answer
Points: 1
Prompt: Record the heading you used to reach the baby and the heading you used to return to the launch zone.
Grading guide: Full credit if both headings are included and match the completed rescue evidence.

Question 6: River Rescue Evidence
Type: File upload
Points: 2
Prompt: Upload a screenshot showing a completed Level 3 rescue with the mission status, telemetry, and rating visible.
Grading guide: Full credit if the screenshot shows the successful Level 3 result and readable telemetry.

Question 7: Why Aim Upstream
Type: Essay
Points: 2
Prompt: Explain why a swimmer often needs to aim upstream instead of straight across the river.
Grading guide: Full credit explains that the current adds downstream velocity, so aiming upstream can cancel or reduce drift and produce the desired ground path.

Question 8: Current Strength Reflection
Type: Essay
Points: 1
Prompt: Predict what would happen to the required heading if the river current speed increased but swim speed and river width stayed the same.
Grading guide: Full credit predicts that the swimmer needs a stronger upstream angle or higher swim speed to compensate for the larger downstream current.
