RIVER RESCUE
UNIT 2 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Race a current, intercept a drifting baby, and thread a safe route between river hazards! You will use vector components and relative motion to solve four increasingly demanding rescues.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Add swimmer and current velocity vectors to predict a ground-frame path.
- Separate across-river speed from downstream drift when analyzing crossing time.
- Use relative velocity to explain interception and collision risk.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Complete the four locked River Rescue levels in order.
2. Open each level DATA panel and record successful heading, speed, time, and stamina evidence.
3. Answer the matching question group immediately after each successful level.
4. Use a successful speed from the safe Level 4 band in Question 10.
5. Compare frames and cite rescue telemetry in the final two responses.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 12 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- No file upload earns or replaces points.
