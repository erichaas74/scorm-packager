Buzz Setup: Unit 6 Lesson 4 Electromagnet Design Lab

What this activity is

This is a self-contained Buzz-native application about electromagnets and circuit design. Students build an electromagnet from a coil, a core, and a battery pack, then lower it into a paperclip pile to measure its lifting strength. A live circuit panel reports total voltage, total resistance, and current, and two constraint meters track the 250 cm wire spool and the 50.00 build budget.

Trials 1 to 5 are fixed controlled-variable trials: each one changes exactly one variable from the Trial 1 baseline, so every student measures identical values and Questions 2 to 6 can be auto-graded. Trial 6 is an open optimization challenge with a 150 paperclip goal. Buzz owns the grading: one upload, five numeric questions, two multiple-choice questions, and two teacher-graded essays. The lab contains no external images, fonts, libraries, or hosted scripts.

1. Nothing to host

Upload the HTML directly as the assessment template. The simulation, evidence generator, and ten ordered Buzz question slots are all contained in electromagnet_lab.html.

If the electromagnet does not draw or the console controls do not respond in student preview, Buzz stripped the embedded script. Host the complete HTML on an approved web server and link to that hosted copy; do not separate the CSS or JavaScript from the file.

2. Create the ten Buzz questions

  1. Create an Assessment titled Unit 6 Lesson 4: Electromagnet Design Lab.
  2. Open electromagnet_buzz_questions.txt and paste the entire file into Buzz's Text editor / Edit all questions screen.
  3. Confirm the import creates 10 questions worth 16 points: one File Upload (3 points), five Fill in the Blank numeric (1 point each), two Multiple Choice (1 point each), and two Essay questions (3 points each).
  4. Preview each numeric question and confirm the tolerance range imported. Buzz's default 1 percent tolerance is not used here.

3. Upload the assessment template

  1. Upload electromagnet_lab.html as the assessment template.
  2. The template contains exactly 10 ordered <a:question></a:question> slots.
  3. Keep the ten imported questions in their created order.
  4. Open electromagnet_lab_preview.html locally to verify the intended question placement before uploading.

Turn OFF randomize/shuffle question order. Buzz defaults to randomized order, but this template fills slots in display order, and Questions 2 to 6 are five similar-looking numeric prompts that only make sense in their labeled slots. Also do NOT use one-question-per-page; students need the lab, the data tables, and all ten response areas on the same page.

4. Preview as a student

  1. Confirm the visible title reads Electromagnet Design Lab and the eyebrow reads Unit 6 / Lesson 4 / Buzz application. A different title means the wrong template was uploaded.
  2. Confirm the electromagnet draws, the sliders and cell buttons respond, the coil and core resize, and the blue field lines appear.
  3. Open Trial 1, select Load required setup, and confirm the console reads 20 turns, iron core, 10 mm, 1 cell and that the note says the console matches the required setup.
  4. Select Run test and record data. Confirm the magnet lowers, paperclips attach, the magnet raises, and the run is added to both tables.
  5. Run Trials 2 to 5 and confirm the measured values match the answer key above exactly.
  6. Run the Trial 6 challenge with 60 turns, iron core, 13 mm, 4 cells in series and confirm it reports 198 clips and clears the goal.
  7. Confirm the progress meter reaches 6/6 and the Download evidence PNG button enables. Download it and confirm all five trials and the challenge appear.
  8. Confirm Questions 1 to 10 appear in their matching labeled slots. If they do not, question randomization is still on.
  9. Confirm Question 1 accepts PNG, the five numeric keys grade correct with the answer key values, both multiple-choice keys grade correctly, and Questions 9 and 10 show essay boxes.
  10. Select Reset all progress after testing so the next student starts with blank evidence.
