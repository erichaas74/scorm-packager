Honors Application: Electric Motor Engineering Challenge

Design, test, and refine a motor system for a defined engineering task. Your goal is not simply to make the motor rotate. Your goal is to use quantitative evidence to improve performance within the constraints.

HS-PS2-5HS-PS3-5HS-ETS1-2HS-ETS1-3

What you will build

A working baseline motor, two systematic investigations, and an optimized final design.

What you will measure

Rotational speed, torque, power, current, and task-specific performance for multiple trials.

What you will explain

Magnetic force, torque, commutation, tradeoffs, design evidence, and the motor’s energy pathway.
