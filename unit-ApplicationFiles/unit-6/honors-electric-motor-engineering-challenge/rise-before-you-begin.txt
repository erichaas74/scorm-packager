UNIT 6 HONORS — BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Electric Motor Engineering Challenge

Read these directions before selecting Begin. This application is your opportunity to apply magnetic force, torque, commutation, energy transfer, and engineering optimization to a working motor model.

YOUR GOAL
Design, test, and refine an electric motor for one engineering task. Use quantitative trial evidence to improve performance while staying within the electrical and material constraints.

BE PREPARED
Have your lesson notes available. Your work saves automatically in the browser, and the application reports progress and completion to Buzz.

WHAT TO DO
1. Choose one engineering task: maximum speed, lifting a load, moving a vehicle, or mechanical power.
2. Adjust the motor controls until every constraint passes, then save a valid baseline design.
3. Select two different investigation variables. Change only one selected variable between trials.
4. Record at least four systematic investigation trials, making sure both selected variables are tested.
5. Use your evidence to create and save a valid optimized design.
6. Complete all six sections of the engineering design log and all nine analysis questions.
7. Review the completion checklist, then select Submit / Resubmit Lab and wait for confirmation.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Export the trial data or print the lab if your teacher asks for separate evidence. Buzz marks the activity complete only after all five checklist requirements are satisfied and the lab is submitted.

When you understand the directions, select Begin.
