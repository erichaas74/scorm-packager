Buzz Setup: Unit 6 Lesson 2 Electric-Field Motion Lab

What this activity is

This is a self-contained Buzz-native application about electric fields and charged-particle motion. Students place and drag positive or negative source charges, inspect the net electric-field arrow grid, and guide a positive test charge through three required challenges. Two additional challenges are optional extensions.

Successful runs are recorded automatically in an evidence table. After all three core challenges, the lab enables a PNG evidence download. Buzz owns the grading: one upload, two multiple-choice questions, and two teacher-graded essays. The lab contains no external images, fonts, libraries, or hosted scripts.

1. Nothing to host

Upload the HTML directly as the assessment template. The simulation, evidence generator, and five ordered Buzz question slots are all contained in dynamic_electric_field_lab.html.

If the field does not draw or the controls do not respond in student preview, Buzz stripped the embedded script. Host the complete HTML on an approved web server and link to that hosted copy; do not separate the CSS or JavaScript from the file.

2. Create the five Buzz questions

  1. Create an Assessment titled Unit 6 Lesson 2: Electric-Field Motion Lab.
  2. Open dynamic_electric_field_buzz_questions.txt and paste the entire file into Buzz's Text editor / Edit all questions screen.
  3. Confirm the import creates 5 questions worth 10 points: one File Upload (2 points), two Multiple Choice (1 point each), and two Essay questions (3 points each).
QuestionKey or scoring requirement
1. Evidence uploadAward 2 points when one legible evidence image shows Challenges 1, 2, and 3 as COMPLETE. Challenges 4 and 5 are optional.
2. Positive source fieldCorrect: To the right, away from a positive source at the left.
3. Negative source effectCorrect: A positive test charge accelerates toward the negative source.
4. One-charge designFull credit requires the source sign and relative placement, local field direction, force on the positive test charge, and a correct force → acceleration → velocity explanation.
5. Curved pathFull credit requires Challenge 3 setup evidence, vector addition of individual fields, the net-field/net-force connection, and an explanation that the net direction changes as the moving charge changes position.

3. Upload the assessment template

  1. Upload dynamic_electric_field_lab.html as the assessment template.
  2. The template contains exactly 5 ordered <a:question></a:question> slots.
  3. Keep the five imported questions in their created order.
  4. Open dynamic_electric_field_lab_preview.html locally to verify the intended question placement before uploading.

Turn OFF randomize/shuffle question order. Buzz defaults to randomized order, but this template fills slots in display order. Also do NOT use one-question-per-page; students need the lab, evidence controls, and all five response areas on the same page.

4. Preview as a student

  1. Confirm the visible title reads Electric-Field Motion Lab and the eyebrow reads Unit 6 / Lesson 2 / Buzz application.
  2. Confirm the field draws, source charges can be added and dragged, the field arrows can be hidden or shown, and Start motion / Reset particle work.
  3. Complete Challenges 1-3. Confirm each success appears in the evidence table and the progress indicator reaches 100%.
  4. Download the evidence PNG and confirm it lists all three core challenges as complete.
  5. Confirm Questions 1-5 appear in their matching labeled slots. If they do not, question randomization is still on.
  6. Confirm Question 1 accepts PNG, the two multiple-choice keys grade correctly, and Questions 4-5 show essay boxes.
  7. Select Reset all progress after testing so the next student starts with blank evidence.
