Create each assessment in Buzz

  1. Create an Assessment and set its maximum score to 15.
  2. Open the lesson's question-import text file and paste the complete contents into Buzz's text question editor.
  3. Confirm the question count above and verify that the authored Score: values total 15.
  4. Upload the matching HTML file as the assessment template. Do not upload the preview file.
  5. Keep the imported questions in their authored order. Turn off question randomization and do not use one-question-per-page.
  6. Keep the two final essay items and their Meta-grading: busybee feedback. Together they are worth 5 points.
  7. Open student preview and complete the checklist below before publishing.

Student-preview checklist

  • The simulation draws and every control needed for the required trials responds.
  • The fixed values shown by the application match the numeric question tolerance ranges.
  • Every native question appears in its labeled slot and the assessment totals 15 points.
  • The lab's evidence table or design log remains visible while students answer the final two questions.
  • Copy, paste, keyboard navigation, browser zoom, and assistive technology are not blocked.
  • Progress stored in the browser does not award points; only native Buzz questions determine the grade.
  • Reset local lab progress after teacher testing so the next preview starts blank.

Student directions to paste into Buzz

Complete the required investigation before answering the assessment questions. Record the specified trials, keep the data table or design log available, and use your measured values in the final two responses. The lab saves work in this browser, but your grade comes from the Buzz questions. Submit the Buzz assessment when every response is complete.
