DYNAMIC ELECTRIC-FIELD MOTION LAB
UNIT 6 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Become an electric-field navigator! You will arrange source charges, launch a positive test charge, and reshape its path using invisible fields and vector addition until it reaches each target.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Predict electric-field direction around positive and negative source charges.
- Connect electric field, force, acceleration, and changing velocity.
- Use vector addition to explain a curved path created by multiple charges.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Choose a challenge. Click + or minus on the arena to add a source, then drag it into place. Use [ and ] to select sources and arrow keys to move them with the keyboard.
2. Solve the first two one-charge challenges by choosing the correct source-charge sign and location.
3. Complete Challenge 3 by combining fields to guide the test charge around the barrier.
4. Use the force-arrow images to identify the mystery charge signs in Questions 1-2. Predict initial motion from rest in Questions 3 and 6, calculate in Questions 4-5, and explain your lab evidence in Questions 7-8.
5. Use the saved signs and positions beside Questions 7-8 in your final responses. Positions are percentages measured from the left and top of the arena.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 8 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- Saved arrangements appear beside Questions 7-8. No evidence download or upload is required.

- BusyBee scoring: Award points only in 0.5-point increments, including whole points, from 0 to the question maximum. Select a rubric-supported allowed score; never report hundredths such as 2.85/3. Do not average or percentage-scale rubric criteria into an off-grid score. A binary completion rubric still permits only 0 or full credit.

4. Upload u6l2-dynamic-electric-field-buzz-assessment-template.html from this folder as the assessment template.
5. Turn question randomization/shuffling OFF and one-question-per-page OFF.
6. Preserve the authored feedback and Meta-grading fields in dynamic_electric_field_buzz_questions.txt; the final two essays route to BusyBee.
7. Use u6l2-dynamic-electric-field-buzz-assessment-template-preview.html for the local placement check, never as the upload template.

PUBLISHING AND PREVIEW CHECKS
- All three core challenges record source signs, positions, and launch/target context. Working arrangements survive challenge changes and same-tab refreshes.
- Field, force, acceleration, and velocity vectors update during a run.
- Completed challenge buttons turn green below the arena. Completing challenges in any order gives an accurate handoff to questions. Start over clears saved progress.
- Questions 1-8 appear in their matching labeled slots and the final two essay questions remain last.
- Keyboard navigation, copy/paste, text selection, and the context menu remain available.
- Teacher-test data is reset before students open the assessment.
- Confirm drag, keyboard, and touch controls all allow students to place and adjust charges.
