UNIT 6 — BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Generator Design Challenge

Read these directions before selecting Begin. This activity is your opportunity to practice the lesson concepts and collect the evidence you will need for the assessment.

YOUR GOAL
Investigate what controls the amount of electricity a generator produces, then use your evidence to design the strongest generator you can.

BE PREPARED
Have your lesson notes available. Use a calculator and scratch paper when the investigation includes calculations or data comparisons.

WHAT TO DO
1. Turn on the faucet, watch the turbine, electrons, and bulb, then stop the magnet and turn it by hand.
2. Change one variable at a time — water flow, magnet strength, coil loops, then coil area — and record each trial.
3. Compare what the light bulb tells you with what the voltmeter tells you over several complete rotations.
4. Build the brightest generator you can, record your final design, and answer every notebook question.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Complete every required investigation step and all required assessment questions. Review unanswered items before submitting. If the lab shows a Submit / Resubmit button, select it and wait for confirmation before returning to Rise.

When you understand the directions, select Begin.
