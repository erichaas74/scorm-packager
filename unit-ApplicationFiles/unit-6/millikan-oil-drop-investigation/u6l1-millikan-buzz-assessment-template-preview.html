Investigation Method

This classroom model keeps each drop’s weight constant and varies its hidden charge. When the drop is balanced, the upward electric force equals its downward weight.

FE = qE = Fg   and   relative charge q/e = 200 V ÷ balance voltage
  1. Adjust the plate voltage until the balance status turns green.
  2. Record the balanced drop, then atomize a new one.
  3. Collect six balanced drops and compare the relative-charge values.
  4. Use the completed table as evidence in the native Buzz questions below.

Model note: The 200 V calibration is scaled for this investigation. A physical Millikan experiment also measures each drop’s size, density, plate spacing, and terminal speed.
