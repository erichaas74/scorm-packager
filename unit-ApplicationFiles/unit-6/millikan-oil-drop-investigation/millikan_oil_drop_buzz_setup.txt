MILLIKAN OIL DROP INVESTIGATION
UNIT 6 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Suspend tiny charged drops in midair and uncover one of physics’ most important patterns! You will balance electric and gravitational forces, calculate relative charge, and use whole-number evidence to reveal that electric charge comes in discrete packets.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Explain the force-balance condition for a suspended charged drop.
- Calculate relative charge from the model balance voltage.
- Use repeated whole-number results to support a claim about charge quantization.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Adjust the plate voltage until the balance indicator confirms that the electric and gravitational forces are balanced.
2. Record the balance voltage, atomize a new drop, and repeat until the evidence table contains six drops.
3. Compare the calculated q/e values and look for the whole-number pattern across the trials.
4. Answer all 10 Buzz questions in order and cite at least three evidence-table rows in the final responses.
5. Export the evidence CSV only if you want a personal record; it is not submitted or graded.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 10 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- Evidence downloads are optional records. No file upload earns or replaces points.
