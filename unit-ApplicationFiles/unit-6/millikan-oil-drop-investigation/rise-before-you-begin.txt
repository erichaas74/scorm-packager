UNIT 6 — LESSON 1 ALTERNATIVE APPLICATION
Millikan Oil Drop: Charge Quantization Investigation

Read these directions before selecting Begin. This application is an alternative to the Coulomb's Law Investigation and focuses on force balance and the quantization of electric charge.

YOUR GOAL
Balance charged oil drops between two electric plates, record the voltage needed for each drop, and use the numerical pattern to explain why charge occurs in whole-number multiples of the elementary charge.

BE PREPARED
Have your lesson notes and a calculator available. Your evidence and written responses save automatically in the browser and report progress to Buzz.

WHAT TO DO
1. Adjust the plate voltage until the balance message turns green. Show the force vectors if they help you compare electric force and gravitational force.
2. Select Record Balance Voltage only when the drop is suspended.
3. Atomize a new drop and repeat until the evidence table contains six balanced drops.
4. Compare the balance voltages and the calculated relative-charge values.
5. Answer all six evidence-analysis questions using values from your table.
6. Review both completion checks, then select Submit / Resubmit Lab and wait for confirmation.

MODEL NOTE
This is a scaled classroom model. The 200 V calibration isolates charge quantization. A physical Millikan experiment also requires measurements of drop size, density, plate spacing, and terminal speed.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Export the evidence CSV if your teacher requests a separate copy. Buzz marks the application complete only after six balanced drops, six written responses, and final submission.

When you understand the directions, select Begin.
