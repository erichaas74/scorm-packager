ELECTROMAGNET DESIGN LAB
UNIT 6 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Find the highest lifting strength per unit of cost. Calculate ratios, compare designs, and justify the best value using measured data.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Explain how current, coil turns, and core material affect an electromagnet.
- Design controlled-variable trials and interpret current and lifting data.
- Calculate strength-to-cost ratios and percentage improvement, then optimize value.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Open Trials 1-5; each required setup loads automatically. Run the test, calculate clips divided by displayed cost to 2 decimals, then select Check calculation.
2. Correct calculations turn the trial tabs green and unlock the optimization challenge after all five are complete.
3. Test at least three different designs in Trial 6, within 250 cm wire and the 50.00 budget. For each, calculate clips / cost and percentage improvement over baseline.
4. Use displayed costs to 2 decimals and unrounded ratios for percentage improvement: (new score / (49 / 14.14) - 1) x 100. Round percentage to 1 decimal. Match the maximum ratio of 6.38 rounded to 2 decimals.
5. Review saved results and checked design comparisons, then answer the 10 Buzz questions. No evidence upload is required.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 10 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- Green tabs require checked calculations. The challenge requires three distinct checked designs and a best ratio of 6.38 rounded to 2 decimals. No evidence download or upload is required.

- BusyBee scoring: Award points only in 0.5-point increments, including whole points, from 0 to the question maximum. Select a rubric-supported allowed score; never report hundredths such as 2.85/3. Do not average or percentage-scale rubric criteria into an off-grid score. A binary completion rubric still permits only 0 or full credit.

PUBLISHING AND PREVIEW CHECKS
- All five fixed trials reproduce the intended current and paperclip results.
- The wire-length, cost, current, and lifting readouts respond to every design control.
- Runs alone do not turn tabs green. Wrong calculations, repeat designs, and high-strength but low-value designs cannot bypass the optimization requirements.
- Questions 1-10 appear in their matching labeled slots and the final two essay questions remain last.
- Keyboard navigation, copy/paste, text selection, and the context menu remain available.
- Teacher-test data is reset before students open the assessment.
- Paperclip count is a classroom strength index, not a manufacturer performance prediction.
