ELECTROMAGNET DESIGN LAB
UNIT 6 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Build an electromagnet strong enough to win an engineering challenge! You will control coil turns, core material, diameter, cells, and wiring while balancing magnetic strength against wire and budget limits.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Explain how current, coil turns, and core material affect an electromagnet.
- Design controlled-variable trials and interpret current and lifting data.
- Optimize a device while explaining resource and performance tradeoffs.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Predict the result of each required change before running the trial.
2. Complete and record fixed Trials 1-5 without changing extra variables.
3. Use the remaining controls to create an optimized design within the 250 cm wire and 50.00 budget limits.
4. Complete the optimization goal, then answer all 10 Buzz questions using the trial table.
5. Cite measured values in the final two responses. The evidence PNG is optional and not graded.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 10 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- Evidence downloads are optional records. No file upload earns or replaces points.

TEACHER BUZZ SETUP
1. Create a Buzz Assessment titled "Unit 6 Lesson 4: Electromagnet Design Lab".
2. Import the 10 questions from electromagnet_buzz_questions.txt.
3. Confirm the import creates 10 questions worth 15 points: five numeric, three multiple-choice, and two essay questions.
4. Upload u6l4-electromagnet-design-buzz-assessment-template.html from this folder as the assessment template.
5. Turn question randomization/shuffling OFF and one-question-per-page OFF.
6. Preserve the authored feedback and Meta-grading fields in electromagnet_buzz_questions.txt; the final two essays route to BusyBee.
7. Use u6l4-electromagnet-design-buzz-assessment-template-preview.html for the local placement check, never as the upload template.

PUBLISHING AND PREVIEW CHECKS
- All five fixed trials reproduce the intended current and paperclip results.
- The wire-length, cost, current, and lifting readouts respond to every design control.
- The optional evidence PNG unlocks only after the fixed trials and optimization are complete.
- Questions 1-10 appear in their matching labeled slots and the final two essay questions remain last.
- Keyboard navigation, copy/paste, text selection, and the context menu remain available.
- Teacher-test data is reset before students open the assessment.
- Paperclip count is a classroom strength index, not a manufacturer performance prediction.
