Unit 6 Lesson 4 - Electromagnet Design Lab

Current design: calculation and strength-to-cost optimization.
Maintained source: electromagnet_lab.html. The .tsx file is the historical source, not the current delivery version.

Student workflow
- Trials 1-5 automatically load their required settings when selected. Run a matching test and correctly calculate clips divided by displayed build cost to turn each tab green.
- Round ratios to 2 decimal places. Use the displayed cost (rounded to cents) consistently; do not use hidden full-precision cost.
- Trial 6 unlocks after all five calculations are checked.
- Test at least 3 distinct designs. Each candidate requires a correct strength/cost ratio and percentage improvement over the baseline. Use unrounded ratios from displayed costs, then round percentage to 1 decimal.
- Formula: improvement (%) = (candidate ratio / (49 / 14.14) - 1) x 100.
- Complete optimization by matching the maximum possible ratio of 6.38 clips per cost unit, rounded to 2 decimals. Different designs may tie at this displayed precision.
- The best candidate is selected by unrounded strength / displayed cost, never by raw clip count. Failed or weaker attempts do not erase completion or replace a better saved design.
- Checked designs appear in the expandable comparison list inside the simulation canvas. No separate completion evidence or download section is present.

Teacher reference
- Fixed trial ratios: 3.47, 3.13, 0.56, 4.69, 2.98.
- Maximum ratio example: 100 turns, 5 mm iron core, 4 cells in series; 181 clips, cost 28.35, ratio 6.38; improvement over baseline 84.2%.
- Another design matching the rounded maximum: 90 turns, 5 mm iron core, 4 series cells; 176 clips, cost 27.57, ratio 6.38.
- Highest raw-strength example: 60 turns, 13 mm iron core, 4 series cells; 198 clips, cost 36.75, ratio 5.39. This is worse value despite lifting more clips.
- The 50.00 budget is a safety ceiling; the wire limit already keeps builds below it. Cost now has an active role as the denominator of the optimization score.
- The strength model is a simplified classroom index, not a calibrated engineering prediction.

Assessment
- Ten Buzz questions, 15 points: five numeric ratio questions, three multiple-choice questions, and two essays (2 and 3 points).
- The final essay compares three designs and checks the percentage calculation and the reasoning behind the best ratio.
- Use electromagnet_buzz_questions.txt and u6l4-electromagnet-design-buzz-assessment-template.html for import. The preview is for local checks only.

Persistence and accessibility
- Version 2 uses a new sessionStorage key so old 150-clip completion records cannot bypass calculations.
- Progress and checked designs persist on reload within the same tab. Reset clears all results. This is not durable LMS completion storage.
- The success dialog receives keyboard focus, keeps Tab navigation within its buttons, and supports Escape. Inputs and buttons use native HTML controls.
