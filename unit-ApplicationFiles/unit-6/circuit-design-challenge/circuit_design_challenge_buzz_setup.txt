CIRCUIT DESIGN CHALLENGE
UNIT 6 BUZZ LAB GUIDE

YOUR MISSION
Wire a reliable three-room electrical system like an engineer! You will measure current and voltage, compare series and parallel circuits, test failures and switches, and defend a design that keeps every room working independently.

WHAT YOU WILL DO AND LEARN
- Apply Ohms law and equivalent resistance to series and parallel circuits.
- Use ammeter and voltmeter evidence to compare circuit arrangements.
- Defend a parallel household-style design while recognizing its current tradeoff.

HOW TO COMPLETE THE LAB
1. Complete the eight circuit missions in order and avoid creating a direct short across the battery.
2. Place the ammeter in series and the voltmeter across a component, then record model evidence for each mission.
3. Test series and parallel bulb failures and all four switch combinations.
4. Build the final three-room circuit and confirm that every design requirement passes.
5. Answer all 10 Buzz questions using the evidence tables. Any downloaded report is optional and not graded.
6. Before submitting, confirm that all 10 questions are answered and the final two responses cite recorded evidence.

SUCCESS AND SCORING
- The assessment is worth 15 points: 10 auto-graded points plus 5 BusyBee points.
- The final two evidence responses are worth 2 points and 3 points.
- Evidence downloads are optional records. No file upload earns or replaces points.

TEACHER BUZZ SETUP
1. Create a Buzz Assessment titled "Unit 6 Lesson 3: Circuit Design Challenge".
2. Import the 10 questions from buzz-assessment-questions.txt.
3. Confirm the import creates 10 questions worth 15 points: five numeric, three multiple-choice, and two essay questions.
4. Upload u6l3-circuit-design-buzz-assessment-template.html from this folder as the assessment template.
5. Turn question randomization/shuffling OFF and one-question-per-page OFF.
6. Preserve the authored feedback and Meta-grading fields in buzz-assessment-questions.txt; the final two essays route to BusyBee.
7. Use u6l3-circuit-design-buzz-assessment-template-preview.html for the local placement check, never as the upload template.

PUBLISHING AND PREVIEW CHECKS
- The circuit builder, meters, switches, failure tests, and evidence notebook respond.
- All eight missions can record evidence and the final design can satisfy all six requirements.
- The report download remains optional and does not affect the Buzz score.
- Questions 1-10 appear in their matching labeled slots and the final two essay questions remain last.
- Keyboard navigation, copy/paste, text selection, and the context menu remain available.
- Teacher-test data is reset before students open the assessment.
- Use meter readings, not electron-animation speed, as quantitative evidence.
