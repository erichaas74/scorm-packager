    Question 9Compare recorded series and parallel evidence

    Buzz Question 9 E - 2 points
    Type: EScore: 2Height: 230
    9) Compare your recorded two-bulb series and parallel evidence. Cite total resistance, total current, voltage across a bulb, and relative brightness for both arrangements, then explain the pattern.
    Always Grading feedback: Evidence must compare recorded series and parallel missions at the same battery voltage.
    A

    Full credit: Cites accurate values for both circuits and explains that series raises equivalent resistance and divides voltage while parallel lowers equivalent resistance and gives each branch the full source voltage. Partial credit: Gives the correct pattern with incomplete data. No credit: Reverses series and parallel behavior or gives no usable evidence.
