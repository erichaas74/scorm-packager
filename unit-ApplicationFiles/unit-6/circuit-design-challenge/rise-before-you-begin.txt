UNIT 6 — BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Circuit Design Challenge

Read these directions before selecting Begin. This activity is your opportunity to build and test circuits, collect meter evidence, and design a reliable three-room electrical system.

YOUR GOAL
Use current, voltage, resistance, and circuit arrangement to explain why series and parallel circuits behave differently. Finish by proving that a three-room circuit meets every design requirement.

BE PREPARED
Have your lesson notes available. The application includes a circuit model, an evidence notebook, and a link to PhET Circuit Construction Kit: DC — Virtual Lab. Your work saves automatically on the device you are using.

WHAT TO DO
1. Complete the eight circuit missions in order.
2. Use the ammeter in series with a path and the voltmeter across a component.
3. Select Record model evidence after each mission, or type readings collected in PhET into the notebook.
4. Test both series and parallel arrangements during the failure challenge.
5. Test all four switch combinations before building the three-room design.
6. Review the Design report and revise any requirement marked with an X.
7. Answer all 27 questions in your own words using measurement evidence.

SAFETY NOTE
Never create a direct wire path across the battery. In PhET, the fire symbol indicates a short circuit or current above 15 A. Use meter readings—not electron animation speed—as quantitative evidence.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Open the Design report. Confirm that all eight investigations, all 27 written responses, and all six final design requirements are complete. Select Submit lab and wait for confirmation before returning to Rise.

When you understand the directions, select Begin.
