UNIT 6 — BEFORE YOU BEGIN
Coulomb's Law Investigation

Read these directions before selecting Begin. This activity is your opportunity to practice the lesson concepts and collect the evidence you will need for the assessment.

YOUR GOAL
Discover how electric force changes with charge and distance, then check the pattern you found against the formal equation.

BE PREPARED
Have your lesson notes available. Use a calculator and scratch paper when the investigation includes calculations or data comparisons.

WHAT TO DO
1. Investigation 1: q2 and distance are locked. Change q1 only and record 5 trials at 5 different charge magnitudes.
2. Investigation 2: both charges are locked equal. Change the separation distance only and record 5 trials at 5 different distances.
3. Investigation 3: predict first, then record 3 combinations, including changes to both charges.
4. Answer questions 1-6 from your own data, unlock Coulomb's Law, then complete the calculation check in question 7.

BEFORE YOU FINISH
Complete every required investigation step and all required assessment questions. Review unanswered items before submitting. If the lab shows a Submit / Resubmit button, select it and wait for confirmation before returning to Rise.

When you understand the directions, select Begin.
