4th Grade Math PBL Units

Unit 1

Explorer Route Math Quest

Students become expedition planners who must prepare a route, supply plan, and travel recommendation. They use whole-number operations, estimation, and remainders to decide whether the expedition is safe, efficient, and realistic.

MAIN SKILLS

Place Value Operations Remainders
Unit 2

Energy Arcade Data Lab

Students become math designers for a science arcade. They test ramps, marble runs, bounce challenges, signal patterns, or chain reactions, then use measurement data to improve the design.

MAIN SKILLS

Measurement Line Plots Elapsed Time
Unit 3

Fraction Trade Expedition

Students become trade record keepers at a global trading port. They compare cargo loads, split supplies fairly, track fractions of goods, and decide which trades are balanced.

MAIN SKILLS

Equivalent Fractions Mixed Numbers Decimals
Unit 4

Hazard City Geometry Command

Students become geometry city planners. They design a safer city map using area, perimeter, angles, lines, symmetry, and shape properties, then defend how their design improves safety and organization.

MAIN SKILLS

Area & Perimeter Angles Shape Properties
