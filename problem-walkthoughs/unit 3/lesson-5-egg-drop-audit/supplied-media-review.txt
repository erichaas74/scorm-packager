3. Crushed Egg Drop Clearance Diagram.png
Use with image 2 as a before/after comparison of reduced clearance. Despite filename, the pictured egg is intact. Describe compressed cushioning, not a crushed egg. Do not infer a stopping time or force from the picture.

4. Compressed Cardboard Egg Cushioning Demonstration.png
Use for cushioning compression or an illustrative later state. The intact egg shows a possible outcome, not proof of guaranteed protection. Avoid calling the base bottomed out without showing that usable deformation travel is exhausted.

5. Egg Drop Compression Sequence.png
Revise before student use. Panel 2 labels the device-to-floor gap 0.05 m; panel 3 uses the same number for internal clearance. These are different measurements. Remove the panel 2 number and show only a very small precontact gap. Panels 4-6 depict egg breakage: this should be an explicitly separate failure example if the animation teaches an intact-egg outcome. Panel 5 says maximum deformation while panel 6 says compressed further: retain just one final maximum-compression state. The approximately zero clearance claim is inconsistent with visible remaining cup/base material. Avoid assigning invented quantitative compression values. Remove step headers if individual frames become Process images.

6. egg_drop_intact_animation.gif
The sampled sequence conveys fall, contact, cushioning deformation and rest without breaking the egg. Fix the persistent bottom label Just before impact, which remains visible after contact and after rest. Label playback as a slowed illustrative model; the six-second playback duration must not be used as the physical fall or stopping time. Keep shell shape/size, container geometry and floor fixed while the cushioning deforms and egg/holder decelerate. The sampled views suggest some overall geometry changes during compression; review motion carefully before publishing. This is instructional animation, not student test evidence.
Preferred Rise treatment: a video block for the MP4, with playback controls, once the equivalent video has been checked. Do not duplicate both MP4 and GIF at the same location.
