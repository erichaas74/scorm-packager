Audit scope and outstanding issues

All 54 original activities were tested in local Chrome at 1100, 390 and 320 pixels, including correct and incorrect selections. No horizontal overflow, clipped SVG text or JavaScript exceptions were detected. The model identity and renderer tests passed. These checks do not establish that every diagram is instructionally ideal.

The 40 retained questions still use the legacy question controller: a 6.5-second reveal, understated correct feedback, the ambiguous incorrect message ?Here is what happens,? and no automatic advance. Some diagrams expose conclusions before the answer. Their group is accurate, but their feedback and pacing still need a separate targeted repair. Several lessons repeat related models; none were deleted.

The converted activities receive separate checks for controls, guidance, completion, replay/reset, reduced motion and automatic demo looping, with desktop and narrow screenshots. Source and extracted upload packages are checked locally; no Rise-hosted verification is claimed.
