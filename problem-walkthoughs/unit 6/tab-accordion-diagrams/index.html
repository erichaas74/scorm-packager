Insulators. In an insulator, added charge does not freely spread across the object.

Attraction of Neutral Objects. Opposite charge is nearer the rod, so attraction can exceed the more distant repulsion.

What is Charge Flow?. Current is the rate at which net charge crosses a section of the conductor.

Temperature. For ordinary metals, resistance usually rises as temperature rises.
