Group 1 sequence, 10 seconds total:
0-3 s: balloon rubs hair; representative electrons travel from hair to balloon. Hair shows positive charge as the balloon gains negative charge. Head fades as rubbing finishes.
3-3.8 s: charged balloon and unpolarized wall reference.
3.8-5 s: balloon approaches; bound cloud centers shift right by up to 8 schematic units, positive centers stay fixed.
5-7.5 s: balloon sticks at wall (center 218 + rightmost surface offset 52 = wall x270).
7.5-8.7 s: explicitly Pulled away, representing external removal.
8.7-10 s: wall returns to unpolarized reference. Automatic loop starts a fresh charging example.

Model: rubbing transfers electrons from hair to balloon; no transfer to wall is modeled, including at contact. Wall net charge stays zero. Far-away reference idealizes negligible applied field. Cloud glyphs represent distributed charge, not literal particle counts or orbits. Balloon movement and arrow lengths are illustrative, not a dynamics solver. Wall contact supplies support during the sticking hold; displayed arrows are electric force contributions only.
