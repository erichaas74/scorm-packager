											68 text Before we dive into designing and evaluating devices, let’s quickly revisit the essentials. Electromagnetic induction is the process where a changing magnetic field creates an electric current or voltage. Devices like motors, generators, and electromagnets all rely on this principle. The way these devices are built—how many coils they have, what materials they use, and how they’re powered—directly affects how well they work. That’s why understanding both the science and the design is key to making technology that’s powerful, efficient, and safe.
