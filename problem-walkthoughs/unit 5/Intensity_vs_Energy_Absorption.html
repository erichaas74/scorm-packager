Key idea: Increasing brightness only increases the number of photons. It does not increase the energy of each individual photon. Only raising frequency does that.
Choose a material and watch how different frequencies are transmitted or absorbed. High-energy photons (X-rays, gamma) can penetrate further; low-energy ones are often absorbed as heat.
Challenge questions:
• Does a brighter low-frequency source automatically become ionizing?
• Why can X-rays pass through soft tissue but not dense bone/metal?
• Do microwaves make food radioactive after the oven stops?
