Unlocking the Nucleus: How Do Atoms Change and Release Energy?

Deep inside every atom lies a tiny, powerful core—the nucleus. Though invisible to the naked eye, changes within the nucleus can unleash enormous amounts of energy, shaping the universe and powering technologies from smoke detectors to the Sun itself. Understanding how and why nuclei change is key to unlocking the secrets of both natural wonders and human-made marvels.

In this lesson, you'll explore the hidden world of atomic nuclei and the dramatic changes they can undergo. By the end, you'll be able to:

1

Describe the structure of atomic nuclei and isotopes.

2

Model alpha, beta, and gamma decay, tracking proton and neutron changes.

3

Explain half-life as a probabilistic population process.

4

Compare fission and fusion, and track changes in atomic and mass numbers.

5

Use evidence to distinguish among nuclear processes.
