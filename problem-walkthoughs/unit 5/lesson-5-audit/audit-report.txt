UNIT 5 LESSON 5 AUDIT
Course: Modern Physics Models: Evidence-Based Approaches to Light and Nuclei
Rise: https://rise.articulate.com/authoring/YJ3eW_Dfj9IPK4twBG8F7mAjE2uw01F4
Scope: all three sections, all tab/accordion panels, process text and native equations, 14 standard multiple-choice keys, final multiple-response key, and sorting activity design. Representative diagrams inspected visually. Audit only; no authoring edits or publishing.

OVERALL
The topic coverage is broad, but the lesson needs revision before student use. The most serious problem is a malformed final assessment which marks false physics statements as required correct answers. New worked examples are much stronger than the older text-only walkthroughs, but are appended after closing paragraphs instead of integrated into instruction and practice.

PRIORITY 1: CORRECT ERRORS
1. Selecting Scientific Models > final multiple-response question: 'Match each observation to the correct model and explain your reasoning.' Five entire question-and-answer statements are checkbox options. Live test selecting 1, 3, 5 returned Incorrect, and Rise explicitly marked the two unchecked false statements 2 and 4 'Incorrectly unchecked'. The key requires all five statements.
   Statement 2 and feedback incorrectly say the photoelectric effect reveals limits of the particle model. The introductory photon model explains the threshold that the classical continuous-wave model cannot.
   Statement 4 and feedback incorrectly invoke element changes in gamma emission. Gamma emission does not change atomic or mass number.
   Replace with separate evidence-based questions; include a genuine explanation prompt or a claim-evidence-reasoning activity.
2. Nuclear Models > Fusion in the Sun > Nuclei Fuse: preceding step names two protons, next step says they form a heavier nucleus 'such as helium'. This falsely implies direct two-proton-to-helium fusion. Show the proton-proton chain at an age-appropriate level: first deuterium, later helium-3, ultimately helium-4; net four protons form helium-4 with other emitted particles. Describe tunnelling simply if explaining why fusion occurs at solar temperatures.
3. Nuclear Models > Binding energy flashcard: 'energy needed to hold the nucleus together' is incorrect. Define as energy required to separate a nucleus completely into its protons and neutrons, equal to energy released on assembling it from those free nucleons.
4. Wave-Particle Duality > Increasing Frequency: 'red light cannot, no matter how bright' is not universal; threshold depends on the surface. Specify a material/work function and the introductory single-photon regime. Replace 'more likely to eject' with clear threshold and maximum-kinetic-energy reasoning. 'Increasing frequency does not change number of photons' requires specifying fixed photon flux; at fixed intensity, higher photon energy means fewer photons per unit area per second.
5. Blueshift questions: section 1 correct feedback says 'Only the frequency and color change'; wavelength changes too. Section 3 keyed option says 'only the observed frequency changes', while its feedback correctly includes wavelength. Use 'frequency increases, wavelength decreases, speed in vacuum remains c'.

PRIORITY 2: REPAIR LEARNING ACTIVITIES AND FLOW
6. Nine older Process blocks contain no images in any actual step (32 steps total): Double-Slit Interference; Single-Photon Buildup Simulation; Photoelectric Effect Simulation; Nucleus Tracker; Starting with 100 Unstable Nuclei; Fission Chain Reaction; Fusion in the Sun; Spectral Lines and Motion; Connecting Nuclear and Electromagnetic Models in the Sun's Story. These are text carousels, not functioning simulations. Some instructions say to select an isotope or adjust frequency/intensity despite providing no such controls. Replace with actual interactions or concise illustrated native Process steps; rename honestly if remaining static.
7. All five newer worked examples occur after a concluding/transition paragraph. Move photon example into photoelectric teaching, nuclear balancing into decay, half-life into population decay, spectral-shift into spectra. Put timing/range in a clearly optional extension or relocate to communication/navigation content.
8. Nuclear sorting has overlapping bins ('Changes element', 'Emits particles', 'Emits energy only', 'Requires neutron absorption'). Fission fits multiple features; neutron absorption is not required for every form of fission. Replace with mutually exclusive described events -> alpha, beta-minus, gamma, induced fission, fusion, or use a multi-feature table rather than one-bin sorting.
9. Section 3 'Match Scenarios with Models and Evidence' is configured as sorting isolated phrases into Observation / Best Model / Supporting Evidence / Model Limitation. It does not connect each scenario to its model, evidence and limit. 'Requires extremely high temperatures and pressures' is a reaction condition, not inherently a limitation of a model. Replace with a structured scenario table or four short cases. Remove the Big Bang/expanding-universe option unless cosmological redshift has been taught; stellar radial Doppler shifts do not by themselves establish cosmic expansion.
10. Too much recall, too little transfer. Most standard questions repeat the immediately preceding statement. Add new-data calculation checks after worked examples, graph interpretation, and model-discrimination questions based on observations. All 14 standard MC blocks accepted intended choices during testing, but this does not resolve vague stems/overlapping distractors.
11. The 'brighter light' question offers two plausible 'No' options; state fixed frequency and use distinct predictions for photon energy, photon arrival rate and maximum electron kinetic energy. The 'extremely bright low-frequency' stem should explicitly say below the given surface's threshold and ordinary single-photon conditions.

PRIORITY 3: PRECISION, IMAGES, ACCESSIBILITY, CONCISION
12. All 17 tab/accordion panels have image assets. Retain them. The missing image problem is concentrated in old Process blocks.
13. Nuclear power Process has five step images, but the six images including its intro all have empty alt attributes. Representative step 1 and step 3 images repeat their step headings, contrary to the requested design. Step 1's bracket groups emitted neutrons with 'fission fragments'; label neutrons and daughter nuclei separately. Explain that fast products transfer energy to surrounding material, producing thermal energy. State that the heat-exchanger sequence is a simplified reactor design, not universal to every reactor.
14. Photon worked example image loads, and its native equation renders correctly. Improve the meaning of the unlabeled colored energy bars with a legend/clear labels: photon energy, work function, remaining kinetic energy. Keep calculations in native text and remove headers embedded in older images.
15. Label Beta Decay as Beta-minus Decay. Include the antineutrino consistently, as the newer example already does.
16. Polarization explanation should say transverse electric-field component along the transmission axis. 'Only waves' is too absolute; distinguish the classical models being compared and acknowledge the quantum model also accounts for polarization.
17. Spectra need an energy-level diagram explaining absorption and emission, not only 'fingerprint' vocabulary. Show an electron gaining a matching photon energy and falling to emit a photon. State rest-frame comparison and radial motion in Doppler cases; no shift does not prove no sideways motion. The newer spectral example handles this correctly.
18. Keep half-life's statistical framing; replace absolute statements about exactly half with 'approximately' or 'expected for a large population'. Stable means no spontaneous radioactive decay, not immunity to externally induced nuclear reactions.
19. Fusion waste claims should be qualified: suitable designs may reduce long-lived waste compared with fission, but neutron activation and radioactive tritium can still matter. Explain control rods absorb neutrons; moderators slow them, which is a different role.
20. Reduce repeated model introductions, duplicate scientific-model/evidence/domain flashcards, repeated Doppler recall, and summary prose that precedes additional teaching. The main formatting issue is structural, not widespread spelling mistakes.

WORKED EXAMPLES TO KEEP
- Photon emission: phi=2.00 eV; 500 nm -> 2.48 eV photon, Kmax=0.48 eV; 700 nm -> 1.77 eV, insufficient. Four illustrated steps with native equations.
- Nuclear changes: U-238 alpha -> Th-234; C-14 beta-minus -> N-14 + electron + antineutrino; gamma keeps A/Z; 0.0200 u -> 18.6 MeV. Four illustrated steps with native equations.
- Radioactive decay: 80 mg, 6 h half-life, after 18 h -> 10 mg. Four illustrated steps with native equations.
- Spectral shift: 500 -> 505 nm, z=.010, radial speed approximately 3000 km/s away; 600 -> 606 nm. Four illustrated steps with native equations.
- Optional timing/range: .10 microseconds -> 30 m; 10 m requires less than .0333 microseconds to remain below budget. Four illustrated steps with native equations.

TARGETED NEW PRACTICE / ACTIVITIES
A. Photon challenge: phi=2.00 eV, lambda=400 nm, use E=1240/lambda. E=3.10 eV, Kmax=1.10 eV. Then double intensity at fixed wavelength: photon energy and Kmax unchanged; ideal emission rate increases.
B. Nuclear balance: identify product of Ra-226 alpha decay (Rn-222), then balance U-235+n -> Ba-141+Kr-92+3n to address the stated fission A/Z tracking goal.
C. Half-life: 120 mg with 4 h half-life, after 12 h -> 15 mg. Pair with repeated random runs from 20 and 2000 simulated nuclei, comparing fluctuation with the expected decay curve.
D. Spectral evidence: rest 600 nm, observed 603 nm -> z=.005, approximately 1500 km/s away; rest 400 nm should appear at 402 nm under same shift. Ask why constant fractional, not constant absolute, shift matters.
E. Photon counter: predict before increasing intensity or frequency; show electron count and maximum kinetic energy separately. Explicitly label material and single-photon model assumptions.
F. Model detective: give a small observation/data set; choose a claim, supporting evidence, and a limitation. For example, individual detector clicks build up a banded distribution: distinguish detection events from their probability pattern.
G. Sun energy path: corrected fusion sequence -> energy transfer through solar interior -> emitted spectrum -> observed absorption lines. Label radiative transfer and convection, rather than vague 'sometimes as particles'.

COMPLETENESS JUDGMENT
All major topic areas appear, but learner performance goals are not fully supported. Missing: real manipulation in advertised simulations, independent numerical practice, actual linked model/evidence/limitation reasoning, and concrete fission/fusion A/Z tracking. Preserve the goals and strengthen these activities rather than adding more exposition.
