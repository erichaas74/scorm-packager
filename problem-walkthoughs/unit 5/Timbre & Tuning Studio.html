Simulation 3: Timbre & Tuning Studio

Explore interference, beats, and the harmonic makeup of complex sounds.

Beat Frequency: 4 Hz
Adjust Instrument B to tune it to Tuning Fork A. Notice the periodic loud and soft "beats" caused by constructive and destructive interference. When the beats disappear, the frequencies match completely!
Adding higher harmonics changes the shape of the wave (its Timbre or tone quality) without changing the fundamental period (its Pitch). This is why a flute and a violin playing the same note sound completely different!
