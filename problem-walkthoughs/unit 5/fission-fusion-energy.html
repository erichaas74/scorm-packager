Model and limits

Expected fissions: N = 10 × kⁿ. k is the mean number of further fissions caused by one fission, after neutron losses. A steady chain has k = 1; it does not mean an explosion. Actual counts fluctuate. This model holds k fixed and omits fuel depletion, geometry, temperature feedback, and heat removal. It does not calculate critical mass or reactor safety.
