About this model

Rover speed 1 m/min; soft sand from 12 m to 15 m; target rock at 20 m. Pictures stream back continuously; Earth decides instantly when a picture arrives. In soft sand the wheels keep turning but the rover moves only 4 cm per minute of driving and sinks. The slip check compares wheel turns with camera motion and stops after 30 s of slipping. Playback fast-forwards while signals travel and slows while the rover moves. Circular orbits; c ≈ 300,000 km/s. Schematic sizes; not an actual rover procedure. The 2005 Opportunity rover event (Purgatory Dune) is the real-world basis for step 2.
