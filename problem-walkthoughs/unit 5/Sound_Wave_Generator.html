Simulation 1: Spherical Sound Wave & Particle Motion

Unit 5 Lesson 2 · Part 1 · Spherical Mechanical Waves

Compression Band Rarefaction Band Air Particle
A central speaker array pushes air molecules outward in all directions. This creates expanding spherical waves—concentric circles of crowded compressions and spread-out rarefactions. Particles only oscillate locally; energy travels outward.
