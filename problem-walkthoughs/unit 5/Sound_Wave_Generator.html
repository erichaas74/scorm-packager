A vibrating source (tuning fork / speaker) pushes air molecules, creating alternating compressions (crowded particles) and rarefactions (spread-out particles). Particles only move back and forth — energy travels forward.
Try these questions:
• Does sound travel in empty space?
• Do the air particles travel from the source to the listener?
• Are sound waves transverse or longitudinal?
