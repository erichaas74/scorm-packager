Unit 5 Lesson 2 · Part 1 · How medium & temperature affect sound speed

343 m/s
Air at 20 °C
Sound travels by particles bumping into neighbors. In solids the particles are tightly bound and springy → fastest. Liquids are intermediate. Gases are slowest because molecules are far apart.

Air (20 °C)~343 m/s
