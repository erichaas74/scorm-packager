Which of these communication technologies uses electromagnetic waves to transmit information?

Correct!

Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, cellular, satellite, fiber optic, and TV remotes all use electromagnetic waves. Some move through air, and some travel through materials like glass fiber.

That technology does use EM waves, but is it the only one? Try again.

How are they different?

Electromagnetic communication systems differ mainly in frequency and wavelength. Lower frequencies often travel farther, while higher frequencies can carry more information but are easier to block.

Lower Frequency • Longer Wavelength

These waves tend to travel farther and pass through obstacles better, but usually carry less data per second.

Higher Frequency • Shorter Wavelength

These waves can carry lots of data quickly, but they usually have shorter range and are blocked more easily.

Bluetooth
Best within about 10 m. Nearby personal devices.
Wi-Fi
Best across a room, home, or building near a router.
Cellular
Best across kilometers while moving through tower coverage.
Satellite
Best for remote locations with no nearby ground network.
Fiber Optic
Best for huge amounts of data over long distances through cable.
TV Remote
Best over a few meters with a clear line of sight.
