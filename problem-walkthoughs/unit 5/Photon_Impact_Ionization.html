Simulation 2: Photon Impact & Ionization Threshold

Part 2 · What happens when photons hit matter?

Below threshold
Typical ionization energy ≈ 10–15 eV
Low-energy photons (radio, microwave, IR, visible) can only heat or excite electrons temporarily. High-energy photons (UV, X-ray, gamma) can knock electrons completely free → ionization.
Misconceptions to challenge:
• Do microwaves change atomic nuclei?
• Do microwaves make food radioactive?
• Are all forms of radiation equally dangerous?
• Does a brighter low-frequency source become ionizing?
