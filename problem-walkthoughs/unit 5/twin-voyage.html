Model, units, and limits

c = 1 light-year per year. Star distances are approximate and measured in the Earth frame. The star and Earth are at rest relative to each other in this model. Your own clock always feels normal.

Constant cruise speed on each leg; acceleration and turnaround take negligible time. Gravity, fuel, and engineering limits are omitted. One-way ages compare simultaneous events in the Earth frame; the twins are apart. A round trip compares clocks together at reunion. The traveler changes inertial frames at turnaround.

Each animation takes 8 seconds, regardless of travel time. Route geometry is schematic. Reduced-motion settings show the completed trip immediately. Reset all restores the starting settings and clears the comparison.
