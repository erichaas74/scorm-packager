Refraction: Snell's Law

Change the media and incident angle to see speed, wavelength, and direction respond.

n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂
Refracted angle28.1°
Speed ratio v₂/v₁0.67
Wavelength ratio λ₂/λ₁0.67
Direction changeToward normal

The wave enters a higher-index medium, slows down, and bends toward the normal. Its frequency stays constant while its wavelength becomes shorter.
