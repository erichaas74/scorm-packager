Group 3: guided simulation. Students vary speed (0.01–0.999c), destination distance (0.1–100 light-years), and one-way/round-trip mode.
Relationship: Earth-frame travel time = path length / speed; traveler proper time = Earth time * sqrt(1-beta^2). Round trip doubles both cruise intervals. Same speed on both legs; instantaneous idealized turnaround, negligible acceleration duration and gravity. Stars are stationary in the Earth frame. One-way comparisons explicitly use Earth-frame simultaneity; reunion is a local clock comparison.
Flow: inspect baseline (Alpha Centauri AB 4.37 ly, 0.5c); change only speed and compare with the last completed run; select round trip and inspect aging at reunion; free distance exploration. Student-controlled Next idea; no automatic step advancement.
Visual evidence: Earth-frame ship position, labeled direction/turnaround, two accumulated-age rulers sharing the Earth-total scale, projected endpoint ages, and the last completed trial. Identity: Earth blue, traveler purple; labels and swatches supplement color. Geometry is schematic; animation normalizes every trip to eight seconds. Reduced motion skips travel animation without suppressing results.
Completion: at least two completed runs with different speeds, including a round trip; send completion once for this document lifetime, even after Reset.
Presets: Alpha Centauri AB 4.37 ly and Sirius 8.6 ly (approximate); custom distance supported. NASA sources checked 2026-10-16: https://science.nasa.gov/sun/facts/ and https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/measuring-white-dwarf-star/
