											70 heading What is a Wave?, Value: 2
												71 text What is a Wave?
											72 text A wave is a disturbance that transfers energy from one place to another, but it doesn’t carry matter along with it. Imagine flicking one end of a rope, the pulse travels down the rope, but the rope itself stays in place. This is the magic of waves: energy moves forward, while the material only moves back and forth or up and down in its spot.
