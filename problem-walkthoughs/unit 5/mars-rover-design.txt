Driving a Mars rover from Earth: guided simulation (Group 3) with one prediction question in step 1. Preserves the established rover artwork, the Mars-now / Earth-latest-picture views, the ghost rover, and the signal lane.
Teaching goal: radio (an EM wave) crosses space at c, so every command plus its picture feedback costs a round trip of 2d/c. Long blind commands are fast but risky; short command-and-check steps are safe but slow; onboard checks remove the wait.
Step 1 (prediction): student presses Send DRIVE 5 m; the command waits at Earth while the student answers "When will Earth see the rover start moving?" (Right away / one-way time / round-trip time; correct = round trip). Feedback ✓ CORRECT / ✕ NOT QUITE with the reason, finite reveal animation, 3 s hold (paused while hidden), automatic advance. No Next.
Step 2 (decision): drive to a target rock at 20 m; soft sand 12-15 m. From Earth the sand reads "Ripple: firm or soft?" until a picture is taken within 3 m. Plans: one long command DRIVE 20 m (wheels keep turning, rover moves 4 cm per wheel-metre, sinks to the hubs; result cites Opportunity, Purgatory Dune, 2005, ~5 weeks to free) and short steps DRIVE 5 m then wait for a picture (the 10 m picture shows sand; Earth stops; 4d/c + 10 min for 10 m). Step advances after both plans have been run, in either order.
Step 3: DRIVE 20 m with slip check: rover detects slip 30 s after entering sand and stops itself; result notes slip checks were added after 2005. Completion message once, after the slip-check run in step 3, even after Reset.
Physics: circular orbits, c = 300000 km/s, distance slider 79-377 million km, 1 m/min. Earth view shows the rover at t - d/c (continuous delayed pictures); lane shows picture pulses every 2 min and red command pulses labelled with the command text. Runs end when Earth first sees the outcome. Playback 10 mission min/s while waiting, 2.5 min/s while the rover moves; reduced motion jumps to the end state.
Evidence: status on each view, Moved X m and (when slipping) wheels turned Y m, 5 m scale bar (hidden at 300 px viewBox when the wheels label needs the space), ghost rover gap "Earth's view: X m behind", sand spray and burial mound, lane equation d ÷ c = t. Durations 'X h Y min Z s'; clocks mm:ss. Controls: plan buttons with command-shaped icons, Replay, Reset, distance slider (locked while running or answering). Optional "Where are Earth and Mars?" orbit panel.
Rover artwork (local units, scaled by k): Curiosity-style side view with rocker-bogie suspension (rocker pivot under the body, bogie to the rear and middle wheels), blue body box with a white deck, high-gain dish and UHF rod, mast with camera head facing forward, angled finned RTG at the rear, stowed arm with turret at the front. Wheels keep x = -23, 0, 23, radius 7, ground contact y 19 and front edge x 30, so motion and measurements are unchanged; grousers and curved spokes rotate with distance driven. When sinking, a soil-coloured band and sand heaps around each wheel are drawn in front of the rover and its shadow is dropped. Interface text is kept to one short line per goal and result; no step chips or status line (fast-forward shows beside the Mars clock). Keyboard focus is restored to the matching button after each redraw.
