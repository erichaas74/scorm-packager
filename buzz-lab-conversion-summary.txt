Purpose

- Students complete the simulation, collect data, upload evidence images, and answer Buzz assessment questions.

- Needs fixed mode: can become numeric auto-graded if a locked preset/Buzz mode is added.
- Already handled: existing question/template work exists or the user said it is done.

Unit 2 Lesson 5 - Inertia and Tension Demo
Summary:
- Level 1 is the cup, index card, and penny demo. During a slow pull, friction carries the penny with the card; during a quick pull, inertia keeps the penny nearly at rest so it drops into the cup.
- Level 2 is the string-tension demo. Students observe a mass hanging between two strings and compare what happens during a slow pull versus a fast pull.
- In Level 2, Slow Pull breaks the top string because the top string must support the 9.8 N weight plus the 15.0 N pull, giving about 24.8 N of top tension.
- In Level 2, Fast Pull breaks the bottom string because inertia keeps the mass from responding immediately, causing the bottom string tension to spike to about 45.0 N.
- Key ideas include inertia, tension, weight, force transfer, and Newton's First Law.
Buzz fit:
- Strong Buzz-native fit. The demo has fixed observable results, and the self-contained template embeds the canvas lab directly above the native Buzz questions with a stage bar to switch between Level 1 and Level 2.
